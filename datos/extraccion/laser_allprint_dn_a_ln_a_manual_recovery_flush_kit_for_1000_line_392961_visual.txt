Flush kit 392961 is a tool for manually clearing clogs in the valve deck and nozzle deck. This is an
additional troubleshooting and recovery option for V ideojet CSEs. The kit includes various manifold
connections that attach directly to the printhead and allow fluid to flush through the Nozzle or V alve
Deck. This kit must be used after running the V AN Sequence scratchpad if that procedure does not
resolve clogs and manual troubleshooting/recovery is necessary for the Nozzle and V alve deck.

Perform preliminary steps for the symptomatic deck before deploying the kit.
Nozzle Deck Clogs
1. Use the V AN.SPS sequence. (See KB Article V AN Scratchpad Sequence - 1000 Series or
Service Packet PDF ).
2. If issues are not resolved, continue with the use of Kit 392961.
V alve Deck Clogs
1. Perform a Hardware Self-test (Calibrate menu).
2. If any printhead valves fail the Continuity test, troubleshoot the printer's electrical system to
determine root cause (See Checking Power Supply and CSB V oltages ).
3. If any printhead valves fail the Open/Close V alve T est, continue with the use of Kit 392961.
