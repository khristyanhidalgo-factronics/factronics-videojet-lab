1.1 Side Filter
NoteAlways exchange the mats of both filters!
1.Remove the side filter cover inserting a narrow screw driver into the grooves from belowand levering carefully.
2.Remove the filter mat.
3.Insert the side cover with new filter mat and let the locking mechanism snap in place.Make sure the groove is pointing downwards.
