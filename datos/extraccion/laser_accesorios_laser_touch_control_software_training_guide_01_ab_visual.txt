Safety
The laser system must only be used for the treatment of material surfaces. The surfaces are locally heated by intensive class 4
laser radiation and are modified thereby. These systems are mainly applied for marking of product surfaces (expiry dates, batch
printing, serial numbers, etc.).
The radiation produced by the laser system is of high energy and therefore improper use represents a danger to persons or
objects!
It is the policy of Videojet Technologies, INC to manufacture non- contact printing/coding systems and ink supplies which must
meet high performance and reliability quality standards. Videojet Technologies, INC enforces strict quality control techniques to
eliminate the potential for defects and hazards in its products.
The intended use of Videojet Technologies, INC Laser Marking Systems is to print information directly onto a product. Use of this
equipment in any other fashion may lead to serious personal injury.
The safety guidelines provided here are intended to educate all attending the presented course on all safety issues such that they
may install, operate, service, and maintain the subject printer (s) in a safe manner.

Electrical Safety Guidelines
This section explains the safety guidelines related to electrical power supply, electrical cables, fuses, bonding, and grounding.
Electrical Power Supply
Personal Injury
Lethal voltages are present within this equipment when it is connected to the mains electrical supply. Only
trained and authorized personnel must carry out the maintenance work.
Personal Injury
Observe all statutory electrical safety codes and practices. Unless it is necessary to run the printer, disconnect
the printer from the mains electrical supply before removing the covers or attempting any service or repair
activity. Non- adherence to this warning can result in death or personal injury.
Personal Injury
A high AC voltage is present at the inverter and backlight. Extreme caution is required when diagnosing failure
in these areas.
Electrical Cables
Personal Injury
Use only the mains power cable supplied with the printer. This cable must terminate in an approved, three-
pole, mains plug which has a protective ground conductor.
l The electrical power cables, sockets, and plugs must be kept clean and dry.
l For pluggable equipment, the socket- outlet must be installed near the equipment and must be easily access -
ible.
Personal Injury
Always inspect the cables for damage, wear, corrosion, and deterioration. Make all grounding/bonding con -
nections void of areas of paint, ink build- up, and corrosion.

Bonding & Grounding
Personal Injury
The printer must be connected only to an AC power supply that has a protective ground conductor and must
be according to IEC requirements or applicable local regulations.
Personal Injury
Do not use the printer if there is any interruption in the protective ground conductor or if the protective ground
conductor is disconnected. The failure to follow this warning can cause an electrical shock.
Personal Injury
Always ground conductive equipment to an earthing electrode or to the building grounding system with
approved cables as per NEC standards in order to drain all potential static discharge. For example, a metal ser -
vice tray to earth ground.
Personal Injury
A resistance reading from the grounded service tray to the equipment chassis or mounting bracket should be 0
to less than 1 ohm. A resistance check should be made using a safe and reliable ohmmeter and should be
done on a frequent basis.
Personal Injury
The PCBs contain static sensitive devices. A suitably grounded, antistatic wrist strap must be worn when work -
ing on or handling PCBs.
Personal Injury
Always prevent static discharge from occurring. Use proper Grounding and Bonding methods. Only use Video -
jet approved metallic service trays and ground cables.
Personal Injury
Always bond conductive equipment together with approved cables to maintain them at the same potential
and minimize static discharge. For example, printhead to metal service tray.

TU430 Overview
TU430 Overview
The TU430 is a touchscreen for use only with TCS+, which runs only on Multi- Core Chameleon boards. There is no processing on
board the TU430, so the unit cannot run the legacy, PC- based versions of TCS software.
Laser units connect to the TU430 through a specialized HDMI connector and cable which carry both signal and power for the
touchscreen.
Possible Equipment Damage - Powered HDMI Port
Never plug a standard monitor into the laser HDMI port as voltage at the HDMI port will damage the monitor.
TU430 is a passive display to interface with the TCS+ program running on the Quad Core Chameleon board. You can calibrate
the TU430 touchscreen via the TCS+ software. See "TU430 Screen Calibration" on page 55 .
There are two versions of the TU430:
l AL- 78550 - TU430 for 3x40 CO2 lasers. Requires a port on the supply unit for connection (AL- SP78053).
l AL- 78185 - TU430 for 7510/7610/7810 fiber and UV lasers. Modifications to the cable allow for connection and grounding
inside supply unit. No external port is necessary.

Cable and Connectors
The cable for the TU430 is replaceable but note that it does not utilize a quick disconnect at the screen side. The cable connector
has an IP65 housing for use with 3140/3340/3640 laser units. The cable connector fits into a connector on the rear of
3140/3340/3640 supply units.
F IGURE 1- 1: (Left) Cable from TU430 with IP65 housing installed. (Right) Connector for TU430 on supply unit of 3x40 laser.
The IP65 housing is incompatible with 7510/7610/7810 lasers as the connector fits directly onto connector on Chameleon. The
IP65 cover prevents the cable from fully inserting to the connector on the board. The AL- 78185 part number for TU430 is spe -
cific to 7510/76107810 fiber and UV lasers. There is no housing on the connector and the cable is stripped so that applying a
cable tie to hold the stripped portion against the grounding bar inside a 7510/7610/7810 supply unit creates a shield ground.

Hot Plugging the TU430
So long as no external USB memory drive is plugged into the TU430, you may hot plug or unplug the unit from the laser at any
time. However, if a USB drive is plugged into the TU430 hot plugging may cause file corruption on the USB memory drive.
While a USB memory drive is inserted, only plug/unplug the TU430 connector at the laser when the system is off or in Standby
mode.

TouchUS Hardware
(TU420H)
The TouchUS is only compatible with lasers using a single core Chameleon board and CMark version 3.x. Systems with a multi-
core Chameleon board and CMark version 4.1 or newer have TCS+ user interface installed on the Chameleon board itself. TCS+
can be accessed via a web browser or TU430 touchscreen monitor. TouchUS is no longer sold with 3x40 laser systems, but is avail -
able as a spare part.
The TouchUS touchscreen PC (TU420H) is loaded with Touch Control Software (TCS) with which you can create and mark jobs
on the 3340/3140 /3640 laser system. The TouchUS unit has an integrated power and Ethernet cable that are combined into a
single cable and connector. Power for the unit is via this integrated connector which has 8 pins and plugs directly into the 8 pin
user interface connector on the rear of the supply unit.
8 Pol Kit Required
It is important that when ordering a 3340/3140 /3640 with TouchUS interface that you select the optional 8
pol kit as an option or you will be unable to connect the TouchUS .

Clearing Alerts
If an alert is displayed:
1. Touch the i (alert) button on the status bar. A screen is displayed with the current Errors or Warnings listed.

Activate & Mark Job
In order to mark a job it is necessary to first activate the job. Activation makes the job the one to be marked and not just edited.
Activate Job
This procedure assumes a job is already created.
1. Touch the Pen button in the Job pane. A context menu opens.
2. Touch Manage Jobs . The Manage Jobs screen opens with a list of job names.
3. Touch the job you want to activate.
4. Touch the empty circle in the Active column to the left of the name of the job you want to activate. You will be prompted
to confirm activation (press Cancel to activate a different job instead).
5. Touch OK to activate the selected job. The circle next to the job you just activated should now be filled in. This denotes the
Active job.
6. Touch Close to return to the Home screen.
