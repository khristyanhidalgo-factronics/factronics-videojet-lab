2.2 Updating the QMark via MMC card reader
When updating the QMark via an MMC card reader, the MultiMedia card (MMC) has to be re-moved of the respective laser system.
1.Put the DataMaster USB stick into the USB port of your PC.
2.Install the card reader according to the card reader's manual. After thatreader as a new disc drive you will see the card
3.Insert the MMC card into the card reader and open the Windows Explorer
4.Open the drive in the Windows Explorer that refers to the card reader.
5.Save the file database.ydb to your hard disk.
6.Close the drive in the Windows Explorer.
7.Select the drive with the right mouse key and format the MMC card.
8.Open the zip-file from the QMark folder on the USB stick and extract all the files to the MMCcard.
9.Copy the saved file database.ydb to the directory Smarties and overwrite the existing file.
