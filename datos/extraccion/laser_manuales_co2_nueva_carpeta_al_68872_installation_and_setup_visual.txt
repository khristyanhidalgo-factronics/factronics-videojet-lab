1 Installation and Setup of the Laser System Using the Handheld
The following is required for installation and setup of the laser system:
metric set of hex keys
cross-head and slot-head screwdriver
1.1 Installation and Testing of the Laser System
1.Mount the laser system at the dedicated spot.Make sure that the working distance is set correctly.
2.Mount the beam protection.
3.Loosen both screws of the housing cover by a half-turn and open the cover.
4.If existing, remove the bridge from connector X2 and connect the external interlock instead.
5.Connect the handheld.
6.Close the housing cover of the supply unit.
7.Connect the mains line.
8.Switch on the main switch and wait until the initialization of the laser system is completed(white LED is on).
