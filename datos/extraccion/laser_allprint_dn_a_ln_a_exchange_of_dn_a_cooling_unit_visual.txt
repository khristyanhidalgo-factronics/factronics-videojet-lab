1 Exchange of the Cooling Unit
The cooling unit of the DN-A must be exchangedin case of leakage
if the following components are faulty- flow sensor
- cooling water pump- temperature controller
- fan
For exchanging the cooling unihex key 5 mmt the following is required:
socket wrench 7 mmof the accessory kit:
hex key 3 mm (00478)insulated screwdriver (66797)
combination spanner 10 mm (66923)
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be dead!1.Turn the key switch to position »0«.
Remove the key to ensure that the laser system cannot be switched on.
2.Wait until the message »Key switch open« is displayed on the touch screen.
3.Switch off main switch.
4.Disconnect the mains supply.
ImportantBefore exchanging the cooling unit the complete laser system must bedrained (see section "Draining of the Laser System", chapter "Installa-

Exchange of the Cooling Unit
1.1 Removal
ImportantMake sure that no dirt gets into the open cooling hoses.
1.Remove the front panel of the PCM housing.
2.Remove the cooling hoses of the cooling unit:- for the marking unit (1)
- for the liquid cooler (2)
3.Remove the three fixing screws in the bottom plate and pull out the cooling unit until theterminal box is accessible.
4.Remove the screws of the cover of the terminal box and open it.
5.Disconnect the 14-pole female connector from the CTP (Cooler Terminal PCB).
6.Remove connector X 300 by loosening the locking screws and disconnecting the connection.
7.Remove the screws of the female connector and pull it backwards.
8.Push the cover of the terminal box through the opening in the bottom plate of the cooling unit.
9.Remove the cooling unit using the handles.

Exchange of the Cooling Unit
1.2 Mounting
1.Insert the cooling unit into the PCM housing so that the terminal box remains accessible.
2.Push the cover of the terminal box through the opening in the bottom plate of the cooling unit.
3.Mount the female connector.
4.Connect connector X 300 to the female connector.
5.Connect the 14-pole female connector to the CTP.
6.Close the cover of the terminal box and tighten the screws.
7.Push the cooling unit into the PCM housing up to the stop.
8.Connect all cooling water hoses to the cooling unit (see figure on previous page).Observe the color code of the hoses (initial water: blue, return water: red)!
9.Fix the cooling unit using the three fixing screws.
10.Mount the front panel of the PCM housing.
ImportantAfter exchanging the cooling unit you have to carry out the start-up ofthe laser system (see section "Installation and Start-Up" in chapter
"Installation").
