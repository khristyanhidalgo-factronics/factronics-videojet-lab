An issue has been reported in which the idler pulley shaft on 6530 107mm printers sustain some
sort of damage, which then causes the shaft to strip the threads on the baseplate. Figure 1 depicts
the location of the damaged threads.

Because of the stripped threads, the idler pulley shaft can no longer be retained, and the
shaft spins in place, unsecured.
As a result of the symptom above, when in production starts the printer:
Asserts faults, usually Printhead Carriage Position Error
Has poor print quality
