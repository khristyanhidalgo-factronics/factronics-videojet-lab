Adjustment of the Resonator LN-A
1.1 Assignment of the Reflections
1.Take a piece of paper and hold it approx. 20 to 30 cm above the adjusting device so thatthe reflections of the pilot laser are visible on the paper.
Reflection from Q-Switch
2.Carefully rotate the Q-switch by hand on the base plate in clockwise direction to determinewhich reflection on the paper is coming from the Q-switch. It is the lightest point moving on
the paper. Note: There might be another moving point in a distance. That point can be ignored.
Reflection from Output Coupler
3.Move the positioning screws of the output coupler using the adjustment key to determinewhich reflection is coming from the output coupler.
Check whether the laser beam reflected by the output coupler hits the center of the adjustmentaperture.
Reflection from End Mirror
4.Move the positioning screws of the end mirror using the adjustment key to determine whichreflection is coming from the end mirror.
Two points are moving: one clearly focussed and one weakly focussed (larger) point.
Reflection from the End Face of the YAG Rod
5.The remaining point on the paper is reflected by the end face of the YAG rod.
