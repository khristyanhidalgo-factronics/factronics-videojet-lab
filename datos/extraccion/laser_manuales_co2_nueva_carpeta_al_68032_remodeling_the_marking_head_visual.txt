1 Modification the marking head (beam path) for LC100/3120
For changing the marking head from the rectangular to the straight beam path you require:
AL-69633 modification kit SHC 60
Instruction Change the marking head cable
Screw driver 6-edged 7 mm and 5 mm, 200 mm long
RiskThe laser system must be disconnected from the power supply withoutfail before starting with the activity.
1.Turn the key-operated switch in the position »0«.Remove the key, in order to ensure that the laser system cannot be
switched-on.
2.Save the database in Smart Graph-Software, user interface »File«. For a detailed description see the manual of Smart Graph-Software.
3.Turn-off the main switch.
4.Pull the mains plug.
