Leakage Check of the Cooling System DN-A

1 Leakage Check of the Cooling System
After the first start-up of the cooling system the following components must be checked for leak-age. The procedure has to be repeated after 60 minutes.
All components in contact with the coolant are checked.
1.Marking UnitCooling hose connections of the pump chamber
Cooling hose connections of the Q-switchCooling hose connections of the marking unit
2.PCMCooling hose connections of the heat exchanger (in the supply unit)
Cooling hose connections of the liquid coolerThe complete cooling system:
- filter units- internal/external cooling hose connections
- cooling water pump- water tank
