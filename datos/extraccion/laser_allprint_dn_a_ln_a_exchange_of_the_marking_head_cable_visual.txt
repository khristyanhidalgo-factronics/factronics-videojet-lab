Exchange of the Marking Head Cable

1 Exchange of the Marking Head Cable
The following is required for exchanging the connecting cable of the marking head:fixed spanner 24 mm
1.1 Removal
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be dead!1.Turn the key switch to position »0«.
Remove the key to ensure that the laser system cannot be switched on.
2.Wait until the message »Key switch open« is displayed on the touch screen.
3.Switch off main switch.
4.Disconnect the mains supply.
1.Unlock connector X 422 at the marking head and disconnect it.
2.Open the housing cover of the laser head.
3.Disconnect the power supply connector X 418 and the optical fiber connector X 421.
4.Remove the nut, the toothed disk and then the cable eye (W 119).Place the toothed disk back onto the threaded bolt and tighten the nut.
5.Loosen the nut of the cable connection in the laser head and remove the connection.
6.Carefully pull out the cables through the opening in the laser head.

1.2 Installation
1.Carefully pass the cables from outside through the opening in the laser head.
2.Tighten the cable connection and the nut.
3.Put the new cable eye and then the toothed disk onto the threaded bolt and tighten the nut.
4.Connect the power supply connector X 418 and the optical fiber connector X 421.
ImportantThe optical fibers labeled with shrink-on hoses have to be connected to eachother!
5.Connect connector X 422 to the marking head and lock the connector.
6.Close the housing cover of the laser head.
