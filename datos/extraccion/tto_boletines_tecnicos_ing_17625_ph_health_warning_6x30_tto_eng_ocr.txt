Our new line of T TO printers are equipped with a printhead monitoring feature in the software.
E3226 Will be displayed when the software detects worn elements on the printhead.
The printhead health can be viewed in the following menu.

The green DOTS indicate healthy elements . These Will change to yellow when worn and to Red
when
they are completely worn.
Besides normal wear on a printhead also consider the following,
• Dust - Can cause premature wear on the printhead elements.
• Moisture - Can cause the Printhead PCB to fail and Will also report printhead health
issues.
• Static - Historically the build up of static causes the E3226 Warning to appear
prematurely. This is most common with oversize film such as Roofing Shingle wrap.
-theory of operation of the printhead health monitoring system
The printhead monitor is a tool on the 6530 which monitors the resistance value of each resistor
on the printhead. There are over 1000 resistors at the tip of the printhead.
As the resistors wear normally the 6530 tracks each resistor and compares to the last reading
based on the value which the PH tested atfactory install.
-why the warnings are populating
As the resistance changes to an unacceptable value in time the monitor begins to report
"Warnings". This is an indication that the PH Will need to be inspected soon.
