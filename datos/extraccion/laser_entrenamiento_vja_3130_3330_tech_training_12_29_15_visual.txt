Non - ionizing Radiation
Radiation that has enough energy to move atoms in a molecule around or cause them to vibrate,
but not enough to remove electrons, is referred to as "non - ionizing radiation."
Examples of this kind of radiation are sound waves, visible light, and microwaves. (source: EPA)

The human Eye

Ocular Absorption Site Vs
Wavelength

• Cornea
– (1) Ultraviolet and low energy far - infrared radiation can injure the epithelial layer of
the cornea; a condition that is painful and visually handicapping. At lower powers, this
injury is primarily due to a photochemical reaction. A latency period of hours may exist
between the time of exposure and the development of the corneal pathology. Minimal
corneal lesions heal
within a few days, but meanwhile they produce a decrement in visual performance.
– (2) High energy far - infrared radiation is absorbed mainly by the cornea, producing
immediate burns at all corneal layers. An infrared laser can produce a burn resulting in
immediate visual incapacitation and may lead to permanent cornea scarring. Very high
energy can perforate the cornea; this perforation may lead to loss of the eye.

Laser Accidents
Statistically laser accidents are due to several factors:
• Eye exposure unexpectedly during alignment, eye
protection not being worn, equipment malfunction, high
voltage mishandling, non - beam hazards, incorrect
eyewear, inhalation of air contaminants, viewing of
laser - generated plasmas, and in general using
equipment incorrectly
• The most common type of injury is to the eye (around
70%). There have been a few deaths, mostly caused
by electrocution or fires.

Jump speed too high

One noticeable characteristic is skirting of lines. You can almost see the dots in every
line. This could be corrected by decreasing Marking Speed or increasing the Frequency.

Marking intensity
The laser parameter Marking Intensity significantly affects the quality of the marking.
Marking Intensity alters the power of the laser marking system.
The Marking intensity is adjustable from 0 to 100%.

Note the jagged line on “M”. The galvos have not stabilized yet and the laser is turned on.
This could be corrected by reducing the Jump Speed and or increasing Jump Delay.

Curves of characters are thick, but straight lines appear normal

Notice that characters like the “O”, “0” does not close, “M” have short legs.
The galvo is tracing the outline of the character but the laser is still on.
This could be adjusted with decreasing the Laser ON Delay.
On some sensitive materials a very low Laser On delay can puncture the substrate.
This sometimes could be mistaken as Laser off delay.

On Delay too high

already. This can be corrected with increasing the Laser off delay.
