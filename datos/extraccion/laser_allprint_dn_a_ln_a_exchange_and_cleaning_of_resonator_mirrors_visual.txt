1 Exchange and Cleaning of the Resonator Mirrors
The mirrors must be cleaned or exchanged in case ofwater leakage in the laser head
too low output power, caused by burned-in spots or contaminations on the mirror surface.
The following is requiredof the accessory kit:
hex key 3 mm (00478)protection caps (65550)
of the maintenance kit:optics cleaning paper (66294)
acetone (66955)protective gloves(66890)
1.1 Dismounting of the Resonator Mirrors
ImportantIn case of burned-in spots you have the following options:The mirror can be rotated 180° so that the spots are not in the area of the
beam exit.If the complete mirror surface is damaged by spots the mirror must be ex-
changed.In any case the mirrors must be cleaned!
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be dead!1.Turn the key switch to position »0«.

3.Switch off main switch.
4.Disconnect the mains supply.
