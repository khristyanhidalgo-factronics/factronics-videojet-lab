Modification the marking head (beam path) for 10 W Laser System
1 Modification the marking head (beam path) for 10 W Laser System
For changing the marking head from the rectangular to the straight beam path you require:
AL-69633 modification kit SHC 60
Instruction Change the marking head cable
Screw driver 6-edged 7 mm and 5 mm, 200 mm long
RiskThe laser system must be disconnected from the power supply withoutfail before starting with the activity.
1.Turn the key-operated switch in the position »0«.Remove the key, in order to ensure that the laser system cannot be
switched-on.
2.Save the database in the software.For a detailed description see the manual of the software.
3.Turn-off the main switch.
4.Pull the mains plug.

1.2 Dismounting
1.Dismount the marking head cable (see instruction Change the marking head cable).
2.Build in the mirror (with the tweezers) withthe following components (from the
modification kit) in this order in themarking head:
- Sponge rubber- Mirror
- Washer- Screws
3.Carefully remove the plugs from the markinghead cover and unscrew the grub screw.
Place the plug from outside in the opening of themarking head cover, where is mirror now.
4.Loosen the fixing screws of the adapter front plate(standard) and remove it.
5.Take the new adapter front plate from themodification kit and fasten it on the laser head.
6.Carefully take the individual leads of the new marking head cable from the modification kitfrom outside through the opening of the laser head.
7.Screw the screwed cable gland.

1.3 Mounting
1.Carefully lead the individual cables from outside through the opening in the marking head.
2.Screw the screwed cable gland.
3.Connect both the scanner connections and fasten the pilot laser plug with the fixing screw.In case a pilot laser is existing, connect it to the pilot laser plug.
4.Connect the connections of the LED. Note that the red cable of the LED should be connected with the plug for the 24 V supply.
5.Place the cable of the LED at the X-scanner, close the cover and fasten it.
6.Fasten the marking head on the laser head in this position (see figure below).

2.2 Dismounting
1.Dismount the marking head cable (see instruction Change the marking head cable).

modification kit) in this order in themarking head:
- Sponge rubber- Mirror
- Washer- Screws
3.Carefully remove the plugs from the markinghead cover and unscrew the grub screw.
Place the plug from outside in the opening of themarking head cover, where is mirror now.
4.Loosen the fixing screws of the adapter frontpanel, rotate the adapter front panel by 90°
clockwise and again fasten it.
5.From inside place the sealing nut in the opening of the adapter front panel (from themodification kit) and from outside the O-ring and the washer.
6.Carefully take the individual leads of the new marking head cable (from the modification kit)from outside through the opening of the laser head.
7.Screw the screwed cable gland.
