The standard interlock uses a 12 pin connector on the rear panel of the supply unit. In addition to
connecting the Interlock Out and Interlock Return signals to desired interlock safety switch, the T rigger
Enable to 24VDC signal should also be bridged on this connector or the laser will not fire.

The following Figure shows an example of a standard interlock switch connected using the 12-way
DIN plug with cable. Notice that the T rigger Enable signal is bridged with 24VDC (Green wire to Gray
wire). This T rigger Enable signal must be tied to 24VDC either in the cable, connector , or on the
customer interface section of the ACC board in order to fire the laser . Symptoms of T rigger Enable not
being tied to 24V include Start button in SmartGraph being grayed out (inactive) resulting in inability to
fire laser .
There is a jumper plug included with all out of box 3130/3330 lasers that bridge both the interlock and
T rigger Enable signals when this plug is in place.
W arning - Personal Injury
Use caution if this jumper plug is in use. When the jumper plug is connected the interlock is
bypassed and the laser can be fired with the interlock open .
