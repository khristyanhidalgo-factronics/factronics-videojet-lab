The extractor is fitted with an internal transformer tha t
outputs 12V DC providing the i Q system with power.
If the buttons on the front panel are not illuminated or
operational this would indicate a problem with the internal
power supply (provided the main isolation switch is in the
ON position and voltage is proven at this point)
The internal power supply is fitte d with a 1Amp inline fuse
this should be checked to prove it is operational, failing this
arrangements should be made to replace the transformer.
