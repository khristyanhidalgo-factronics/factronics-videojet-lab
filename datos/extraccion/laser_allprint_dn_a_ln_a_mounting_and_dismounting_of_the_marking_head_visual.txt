Dismounting and Mounting of the Marking Head

1 Mounting and Dismounting of the Marking Head
Required tools of the accessory kit:offset hex key 5 mm (66921)
protection cap (65559) for the opening in the marking headprotection cap (66284) for the opening in the plate for connecting the marking head
1.1 Mounting
1.Remove the cap from the plate for connecting the marking head.
2.Remove the cap from the opening in the marking head and remove the two M6 screws ofthe marking head.
3.Position the marking head onto the adjusting pins of the connection plate and tighten it usingthe M6 screws.
4.Connect plug X422 to the marking head and lock it.
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be dead!1.Turn the key switch to position »0«.
1.2 Dismounting Remove the key to ensure that the laser system cannot be switched on.
2.Wait until the message »Key switch open« is displayed on the touch screen.
3.Switch off main switch.
4.Disconnect the mains supply.
ImportantBefore dismounting the marking head puscan lens.t the protection cap onto the laser
If you want to remove the laser scan lens see section "Disassembly of the Laser Scan Lens" of chapter "Maintenance".
The removed laser scan lens must be covered with protective caps on both sides and the resulting opening in the marking head must be closed with a
cap!
1.Unlock the plug X422 at the marking head and disconnect it.
2.Hold the marking head and loosen the two screws of the connection plate.
3.Remove the marking head from the laser head.
4.Cover the opening in the marking head with the cap and close both holes in the markinghead with the screws.
5.Close the opening in the connection plate of the laser head with a cap.
