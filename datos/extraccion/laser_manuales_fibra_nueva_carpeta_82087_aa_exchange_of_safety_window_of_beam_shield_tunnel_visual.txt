Exchange of Safety Windows of Beam Shield Tunnel
2.Side panel without service door:Remove the side panel by removing the screws.
3.Inside the tunnel remove the window cover (if mounted) by removing the two nuts of the cover.
4.Remove the nuts of the frame and carefully remove the frame including safety window.
NoteThe window is held by the frame only. Make sure it cannot fall down.
5.Remove the window from the frame.
6.Insert the new window into the frame, the text on the window must be readable from outside.
7.Mount the frame including safety window by tightening the nuts of the frame.
NoteMake sure that all nuts are tightened crosswise, i.e. tighten diagonally oppo-site nuts!
