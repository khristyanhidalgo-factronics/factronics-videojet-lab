Exchange of Pump Chamber LN-A
1.1 Removal of the Pump Chamber
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be dead!
1.Turn the key switch to position »0«.Remove the key to ensure that the laser system cannot be switched on.
2.Wait for the message »Key switch open« to be displayed on the touchscreen.
3.Switch off the main switch.
4.Disconnect the mains connector.
5.Wait for 6 minutes for the ignition module contacts to be completely dis-charged!
ImportantThe marking unit must be placed above the level of the cooling unit!
1.Open the housing cover of the marking unit.
2.Remove both lamp connections from the ignition module.
3.Loosen first the ventilating screw (6) and then botchamber.h locking screws (5) to ventilate the pump
4.Let the water drain until the pump chamber is completely empty.By loosening the screws the cooling water circuit is ventilated and the cooling water flows
from the pump chamber back to the expansion tankin the cooling water hose. of the cooling unit. Air bubbles are rising
5.Tighten the ventilating screw and the locking screws.
6.Remove the cooling hose connections for the Q-switch (1) and the pump chamber (2).Close the openings in the pump chamber using the protection caps.
7.Remove the beam protection tube between the output coupler and the pump chamber.Push the silicone hose between the Q-switch and the pump chamber onto the dust protection
pipe of the pump chamber.
8.Remove first the grounding screw (3), then the fixing screw of the pump chamber base plate(4) and remove the pump chamber.

1.2 Mounting of the Pump Chamber
ImportantProtect all components in the laser head from dust and dirt using the protec-tion caps.
1.Remove the protection caps from the dust protection pipes and the water connections ofthe new pump chamber.
2.Remove the silicone hose from the old pump chamber and place it onto the dust protectionpipe of the new pump chamber.
3.Place the pump chamber onto the pump chamber base plate.
4.Tighten the grounding screw with two toothed disks as follows:- toothed disk (to the pump chamber)
- cable eye of the grounding cable- toothed disk
- grounding screw.
5.Tighten the fixing screw (4).
6.Connect the hoses (1) and (2) to the new pump chamber.
7.Mount the lamp connections to the terminals of the ignition module.Observe the polarity (red + / black -).
8.Lay the connecting cables in an arc and clamp of the pump chamber.each into a cable holder on the upper part
ImportantMake sure that the cables do not touch the dust protection flange.
9.Make sure that the ventilating screw (6) and the locking screws (5) are tight.
10.Place the beam protection tube between the output coupler and the pump chamber. Place the silicone hose between the pump chamber and the Q-switch.
ImportantAfter exchanging the pump chamber the laser system must be bled(see chapter "Maintenance", section "Bleeding of the Laser System")
and the resonator must be adjusted (see section "Adjustment of theResonator" in this chapter).
