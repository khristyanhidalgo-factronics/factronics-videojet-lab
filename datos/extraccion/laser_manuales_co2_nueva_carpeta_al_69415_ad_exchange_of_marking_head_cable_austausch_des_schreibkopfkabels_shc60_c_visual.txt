AL-69415Service Part Installation Instruction
Exchange of the Marking Head Cable for LC100/3120 (SHC60)
1 Exchange of the Marking Head Cable for LC100/3120 (SHC60)
The following is required for exchanging the marking head cable:
instruction "Exchange of the Marking Head"
offset hex keys 3 mm and 2.5 mm
offset hex key 3 mm, length 200 mm
spanners 22 mm and 24 mm
1.1 Dismounting
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be dead!
1.Turn the key-operated switch in the position »0«.Remove the key, in order to ensure that the laser system cannot be
switched-on.
2.Save the database in the software. For a detailed description see the manual of the software.
3.Turn-off the main switch.
4.Pull the mains plug.
1.Loosen the screws of the housing cover and remove the cover.
