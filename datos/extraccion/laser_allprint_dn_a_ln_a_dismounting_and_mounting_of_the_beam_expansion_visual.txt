Dismounting and Mounting the Beam Expansion

1 Dismounting and Mounting of the Beam Expansion
For removing the laser beam expander the following is requiredof the accessory kit:
2 protection caps for the beam expander (66282, 65559)protective cap for the output coupler (65550)
of the adjustment kit:offset hex key 2 mm (66920)
1.1 Dismounting
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be dead!1.Turn the key switch to position »0«.
Remove the key to ensure that the laser system cannot be switched on.
2.Wait until the message »Key switch open« is displayed on the touch screen.
3.Switch off main switch.
4.Disconnect the mains supply.
1.Open the housing cover of the laser head.
2.Remove the beam protection tube between output coupler and beam expander.
3.Cover the opening of the output coupler with the protection cap.
4.Rotate the shell of the beam expander in counterclockwise direction until it can be removed.
5.If the beam expander cannot be removed insert the hex key into the blind hole and rotatein counterclockwise direction.
AttentionThe laser must not be started without beam expander! Otherwise thegalvo mirrors can be destroyed!
6.Cover the openings of the beam expander using the protection caps.

Dismounting and Mounting the Beam Expansion
1.2 Mounting
1.Remove the protection caps.
2.Insert the beam expander rotating in clockwise direction.Make sure that the reference edge is facing upwards.
Figure 1-1: Mounting of the beam expander
3.Place the beam protection tube between output coupler and beam expander.
4.Close the housing cover of the laser head.
AttentionBefore the first use (spare parts) all beam expanders must be adjusted (see "Adjustment of the Diverging Lens").
It is not possible to exchange the beam expanders between two lasers without adjustment.
