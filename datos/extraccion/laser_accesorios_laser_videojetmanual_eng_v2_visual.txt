2Installation
2.1Introduction
When a component is laser marked an amount of the surface of the substance isthermally decomposed, Òburnt offÓ. This thermal decomposition, comprises a mixture
of particulate and gaseous compounds. The heat energy causes the gases andsurrounding air to quickly expand moving away from the surface at high velocity
entraining any particulate with the gases. This is the fume.
There are two main reasons for capturing the fume
1Operational Ð if ignored the fume can settle on the laser optics causingdamage to the lens and impairing the quality of the marking
2Health and Safety Ð The particulate generated from most materials is submicron size which is a health hazard if inhaled and some materials give off
harmful gases which again operators need protecting from. The AD180 and AD400 are suitable for extracting the fume from laser marking
applications, capturing it in the multistage filter system and returning theassociated clean air back into the workplace
2.2Fume CaptureThe fume is normally captured by one of two methods, either a flexible arm
and nozzle close to the marking point or an enclosure around the markingarea
2.2.1Arm and nozzle extractionFor most applications the product to be
marked, on a conveyor, will move pastthe stationary laser. The nozzle should
be positioned as close as possible tothe marking area on the downstream
side of the laser. (See fig. 1)
Purge air should be kept to a minimum, where possible, to prevent the fume beingblown away from the nozzle
High speed bottling lines may need bigger scoops or nozzles both sides of thebottles because of the turbulence caused by the speed of the bottles

2.4Extractor Installation Procedure
2.4.1Move the unit to the location where it is going to be installed, remove the unitfrom its packaging.
2.4.2Ensure that a 0.5mspace is available around the louvred areas of the unit toensure good air flow. Lock the two castors with brakes.
2.4.3The extraction unit is fitted with a pressure transducer to monitor the conditionof the filters and to indicate the extractor is running. In addition to controlling
the LEDÕs on the front of the unit this signal is available via the green andwhite cores of the four core cable that exits the cabinet next to the power
cable. The signal is a Òvolt freeÓ contact, i.e. a closed circuit will exist betweenthe green and white wires when the filter condition is good and the unit is
running, this will change to an open circuit on filter blockage or system failure.The current limit for this circuit is 3.0 amps. The signal can be connected to
the laser or alternatively to operate a beacon, siren or other warning device.Open circuit condition of this circuit will not directly stop the extractor motor.
2.4.4Remote stop/start. The red and black cores of the 4 core cable need to beconnected to an 18-36volt ACor DCsupply which when applied will start the
unit and when switched off will stop the unit.
2.4.5AD 180 set the voltage selector switch to 230v or 115v in line with theelectrical supply being used (see fig 4).
2.4.6Check the integrity of he electrical power cable
Caution Test the mains input at the isolated supply is the same as thevoltage selected on the switch (item 2.4.50
2.4.7Connect the power cable to an isolated electrical supply.

Maintenance
4Maintenance
Maintenance consists of replacing filters only. Replace the filters annually orwhen the filter change indicator is activated
4.1Pre filter replacement The filter needs changing when the filter change signal is alarmed and/or the
green amber and red LEDÕs on the unit are illuminated.Isolate the electrical supply to the extractor
Caution - When changing used filters always wear mask, safety glasses andgloves.
Undo the two catches, by turning through 90¼, on the rear of the unit andopen the door. The pre filter is the lower of the two filters (see fig 8). Slide the
filter to the left, to disengage it from the inlet connection, then pull thecartridge halfway out of the unit. Peel the cover from the adhesive label on
the side of the filter and stick it over the inlet hole of the filter thus sealing thedust inside the cartridge.
Remove the filter from the extraction unit
Slide a new filter all the way inside the unit then move to the right to locate itonto the suction connection
Close the door, fasten the two latches and re-connect the power supply.

4.2Combined filter replacementThe filter needs changing annually or when the filter alarm signal and LEDÕs
do not go off after changing the pre filter. Isolate the electrical supply to the extractor
Caution - When changing used filters always wear mask, safety glasses andgloves.
Undo the two catches, by turning through 90¼, on the rear of the unit andopen the door. The combined filter is the higher of the two filters (see fig 8).
Slide the filter out of the unit holding tightly to the handles on the filter as it isheavy and will tend to drop when it comes clear of the unit.
Slide a new filter into position.
Close the door, fasten the two latches and re-connect the power supply.
