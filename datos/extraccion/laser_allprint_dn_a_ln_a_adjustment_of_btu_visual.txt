Adjustment of BTU

1 Adjustment of Beam Turning Unit (BTU)
The beam turning unit has to be adjustedif the mirror has been cleaned or exchanged
if the BTU is installedif the output power is too low
The following is requiredof the accessory kit:
hex key 3 mm (00478)of the maintenance kit:
adjusting device, cross wire (91688)indicator disk (94014)
adjustment key (65509)offset hex key 1.5 mm (66919)
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be dead!1.Turn the key switch to position »0«.
Remove the key to ensure that the laser system cannot be switched on.
2.Wait until the message »Key switch open« is displayed on the touch screen.
3.Switch off main switch.
4.Disconnect the mains supply.
ImportantRemove the marking head before adjusting the BTU (see section "Mountingand Dismounting of the Marking Head" in this chapter).

1.2 Adjustment of BTU with Installation of the Laser System
1.Open the housing cover of the laser head.
2.Make sure that the beam expander is tightened firmly to the housing plate of the laser head.
3.Loosen the fixing screws of the mirror carrier in the BTU and remove the carrier.
4.Insert the adjusting device (cross wire) into the beam outlet of the connection plate for themarking head.
5.Position the indicator disk with the coated side facing the laser head approx. 20 cm fromthe cross wire (mount a holder if necessary).
6.Loosen the adapter ring of the beam expander.
7.Rotate the beam expansion module until the counter screws face upwards.
8.Loosen the two counter screws until they are flush with the module surface.

1.2.2 Adjustment of Several Beam Turning Units
AttentionIf you have a system with several beam turning units you have to start adjust-ing the BTU closest to the laser!
1.Loosen the fixing screws of the BTU cover and remove it from the housing.
2.Loosen the fixing screws of the mirror carrier of the next BTU and remove the mirror carrier.
3.Insert the adjusting device (cross wire) into the beam outlet of the mounting plate of the BTUwithout mirror carrier.
4.Position the indicator disk with the coated side facing the laser head approx. 20 cm fromthe cross wire (mount a holder if necessary).
5.Select »Shutter open« in the Smart Graph software.
6.Adjust the current value so that an image appears on the indicator disk and the shadow ofthe cross wire is clearly visible.
