Product Overview
The SureSho t was designed to create a redundant printing system. This device
allows two printers to alternate printing from product to product. If one of the
printers drops from a Print - Ready state with a fault, or is removed manually from
the print mode, the remaini ng printer will assume printing on all products. If both
printers drop from the Print - Ready mode with a fault, or are manually removed
from print, the SureShot triggers an alert that both printers are offline. This
provides the highest reliability over and above a spare printer.
The SureShot works from a single detector with two printers. The printheads
should be located as close to the detector, and to each other as possible. It is
recommended that the printheads be mounted across from each other on eithe r
side of the product or, if this is not possible, then the printheads should be placed
side by side. This will require that the printers have different print delays. The
space between products must be large enough so that the printhead with the
largest pr int delay has completed printing prior to receiving the next product
detect signal.
When used with Excel printers, the SureShot monitors the Print - Ready signal
from pin 4 on the J29 connector of the printer main control board. When used
with Triumph or 37 - series printers, the Sure Shot monitors Pin 3 of the J3
connector on the Print - Ready board included in SAR 00165. If both printers are in
the print mode, the product detect signal will be sent to the printers alternately,
and the printers will print on al ternate products.
Some of the advantages of using the SureShot are:
 The printers are in constant operation and not in idle mode, which is
crucial for maximum print quality and printer performance.
 SureShot gives customer the additional comfort of having an unattended
spare. If one printer fails or is taken off - line, the second printer
immediately begins printing on all products.
 SureShot allows the customer to perform scheduled maintenance on their
printers without having to shut down the production line.
 Downtime due to printer faults is dramatically reduced, very close to non -
existent.

Connecting the SureShot to the Product Detector
Connect the product detector cable to the Sensor Input connect or on the
SureShot module.
The printers will share the same product detector. The product detector will
send the product detect signal to the SureShot, then the print signal will be
sent to the appropriate printer, alternating from one printer to the other . If the
product detector fails for any reason, neither printer will print.
Setting Up Your Product Detector
Turn both printers On .
When using a proximity type detector, set it for the following conditions.
Detect Leading Edge Detector Dark - Light
No Dark
When using a retroflective - type detector, set it up for the following
conditions.
Detect Leading Edge Detector Dark - Light
Yes Light
Setting Up the Print Delay
When setting up the Print Delay, keep the following points in mind:
 The print delay setting on the printers depends on the distance between
the product detector and the printhead plus the distance the code is to
be printed from the product’s leading edge.
 The value of the delay adds to the length of the message. The message
must finish printing b efore the next product is detected.
 The distance between the products must be large enough to
accommodate the length of the message, plus the print delay value. If
the message and delay value exceeds the product spacing, products can
be missed.
 If the abo ve is not followed, a Not Phasing warning, or missed printing
of products may occur.

Fault Output of SureShot
The alarm output of the Sure Shot is a SPDT relay or cold - contact closure
with a contact rating of 50vdc at 2 amps of current. This mechanical
co ntact will close when both printer “A” and printer “B” have faulted out.
The wire outputs of the Fault output are listed below:
Wire Color Connection Purpose
Red Pin #5 Common pin of relay
Brown Pin #4 Normally Open contact (NO)
White Pin #2 Normally Cl osed contact (NC)

Operation of SureShot
The SureShot module has six LEDs (refer to Figure 4):
 Sensor Input
 Fault
 Printer “A” Ready/Not Ready
 Printer “A” output
 Printer “B” Ready/Not Re ady
 Printer “B” output
The LED’s operation is described below (refer to Figure 4):
 SENSOR INPUT is a green LED that displays the triggering of the
product detector. This LED will flash when the products are seen.
 PRINTER “A” OUTPUT is a green LED that il luminates when the
“A” printer has printed last.
 PRINTER “B” OUTPUT is a green LED that illuminates when the
“B” printer has printed last.
 PRINTER “A” NOT READ Y is a red LED that illuminates when
Printer “A” is not ready to print. This can be due to a prin ter fault, or
the manual removal of the printer from the print mode.
 PRINTER “B” NOT READ Y is a red LED that illuminates when
Printer “B” is not ready to print. This can be due to a printer fault or
the manual removal of the printer from the print mode.
 FA ULT is a red LED that illuminates when both printers “A” and
“B” are not in a Print - Ready state. This can be due to both printers
being in a fault condition, or both printers having been manually
removed from the print mode.
