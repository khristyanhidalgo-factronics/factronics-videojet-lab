1.3 Power Verification
1.Log into TCS+/LinxVision software.
2.Open the settings menu and go to Laser Unit Settings.
3.In the Laser Unit Settings open Laser Configurationset to 100%. and make sure Power Correction is
4.Carry out a power check directly under the marking head.
NoteDo not measure the power in the focus point, that would damage the powermeter.
5.If the measured value is 4W, leave the setting as is.
If the measured value is higher, lower the output power by changing the percent setting.
6.Repeat the measurement.
7.Repeat steps 5 and 6 until the measured output power is 4W.
8.Click on Save (keyswitch must be open), then Close the dialog.
