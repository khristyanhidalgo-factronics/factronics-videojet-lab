March 31, 2022
VIDEOJET NOTICE
Important Information on Videojet and Willet Lasers:
Mandatory Laser Software Upgrade to Correct Potential Internal-Communication Error
Videojet Laser Models VJ3320, VJ3120, VJ3330, VJ3130;
Willet Laser Model W850
* *IF YOU STILL HAVE ONE OF THESE LASER MODELS, PLEASE READ THIS NOTICE CAREFULLY**
____________________________________________________________________________________
Dear Valued Customer,
 You are receiving this letter because our records show that you purchased one of the laser models listed
above, which requires a remedial software upgrade. We will provide this mandatory software upgrade to you free
of charge, as explained below. Please forward this letter immediately to any of your production-maintenance
personnel who work with one or more of these laser models.
It has recently come to our attention that the laser models listed above, which employ Qmark-based
software systems, can, under certain conditions, emit laser energy onto a single point for a prolonged period of
time. In some cases, a contributing factor could be a software-communications error which can cause the laser to
stop processing normally while in an activated marking state (i.e., while the beam source is active). While your
laser unit is likely functioning properly, we are providing a mandatory software upgrade to address this potential
communication error in your existing Qmark-based software.
This mandatory software upgrade requires on-site installation by one of our technicians. The upgrade to
Qmark 8.10.1 will recognize potential internal-communications errors and will address them by triggering the
following “fatal error” message: “Incomplete marking due to an internal communications error.” This message
will, in turn, automatically deactivate the laser-beam source.
Please contact your local Videojet service team at your earliest convenience to schedule a technician
visit so that your new software can be installed. https://www.videojet.com/us/homepage/service-and-
support/contact-us/global-offices.html
 If you have already been contacted by Videojet’s service team to set an appointment for this laser-
software upgrade, or if you have already had the upgrade to Qmark 8.10.1 installed, you may disregard this notice.
