The configuration of the mandrel leaf springs on the cassette mandrel(s) is dependent on the width of
the ribbon in use. Incorrect positioning for the ribbon can cause tight core removal 6530, 6330, or 6420
53mm or 107mm printer . Leaf spring position is critical to proper performance.
Make sure the position of the leaf springs on the mandrels are configured correctly based on the size
of the ribbon in use.

P r o ced u r e
1. Remove ribbon from both mandrels.
2. Compare the current spring position to the correct position for the ribbon in use as shown in
T able 1 to determine if adjustment is required.
If positioning is correct, inspect the mandrels for any looseness and proper
rotation.
If positioning is incorrect, continue with the adjustment procedure.
3. Using the 3mm hex key , remove the screw holding the leaf springs in place.
4. Rotate the leaf springs to the appropriate potions based on the ribbon size as shown in T able
1..
5. Carefully re-install the screws and ensure they are secure.
6. Inspect the mandrels for any looseness and proper rotation.
7. Install the ribbon.
T able 1: Leaf Spring Positions by Ribbon Size
Ribbon Size Leaf Spring Positioning
20-30mm
Orient the spring toward the disc and fasten the screw through the hole furthest from
the disc.

Ribbon Size Leaf Spring Positioning
30-50mm
Orient the spring toward the outward end of the mandrel and fasten the screw
through the hole closest to the disc.

Ribbon Size Leaf Spring Positioning
55-75mm
Orient one leaf spring toward the disc and fasten the screw in the hole
closest to the disc.
Orient the second leaf spring toward the outer end of the mandrel and
fasten the screw in the middle hole, immediately adjacent to the first leaf
spring.

Ribbon Size Leaf Spring Positioning
80-1 10mm
Orient one leaf spring toward the disc and fasten the screw in the middle
hole. The spring will cover the hole closest to the disc.
Orient the second leaf spring toward the disc and fasten the screw in the
hole closest to the outer end of the mandrel.
