1.2 Installation of the MIC-HMS
Connector for
MIC-HMS board
NoteOnly the Chameleon MCIP (AL-78900) can be used with a MIC-HMS board.
There are two types of MIC-HMS boards:
PROFINET (AL-79384), yellow sticker on the left RJ45 socket
EtherNet/IP (AL-79426), blue sticker on the left RJ45 socket
1.Plug the MIC-HMS onto the Chameleon board and tighten it with two screws.
2.Connect a PC to the Ethernet socket on the Chameleon board.
3.Connect the laser to mains.
4.Switch on the main switch at the back of the supply unit.
5.Start the laser by pressing the button POWER/STANDBY on the keypad.Wait until the booting process is complete.
