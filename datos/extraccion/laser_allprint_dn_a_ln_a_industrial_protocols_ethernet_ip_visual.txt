V ideojet supports two common Industrial Protocols, the Ethernet/IP protocol is covered in this document.
EtherNet/IP is used primarily with Allen Bradly PLCs.
EtherNet/IP is an industrial network protocol that adapts the Common Industrial Protocol to standard
Ethernet. EtherNet/IP is one of the leading industrial protocols in the United States and is widely used in a
range of industries including factory , hybrid and process.
EtherNet/IP uses two of the widely deployed collections of Ethernet standards – the Internet Protocol suite
and IEEE 802.3 – to define the features and functions for its transport, network, data link and physical
layers. EtherNet/IP performs at level session and above (level 5, 6 and 7) of the OSI model. CIP uses its
object-oriented design to provide EtherNet/IP with the services and device profiles needed for real-time
control applications and to promote consistent implementation of automation functions across a diverse
ecosystem of products. In addition, EtherNet/IP adapts key elements of Ethernet’ s standard capabilities
and services to the CIP object model framework, such as the User Datagram Protocol (UDP), which
EtherNet/IP uses to transport I/O messages. (2019 - Sep Wikipedia - Ethernet/IP).
Note: EtherNet/IP cycle time has not been tested.

The EtherNet/IP must be enabled and configured before the use of EtherNet/IP .
The EtherNet/IP has its own part number and is specific to this protocol. It is not possible to change the
firmware and software if the wrong part is ordered, the solution is simply to order the correct board.
Following the installation of the board, complete the following steps to enable and configure the board.
1 . Connect to W eb Interface .
2 . Select on Other icon from the home screen menu.
3 . Select External Interface f rom the drop-down menu.
4 . Select the Other icon and select the External Interface icon from the dropdown selector .
5 . Select the EtherNet/IP selection and click Save to save the configuration setting.
6 . Leave Use DHCP setting toggled ON to use DHCP .
-OR-
Manually configure the network settings:
1 . Click to toggle DHCP to OFF .
2 . Enter the static IP for the Ethernet/IP board.
