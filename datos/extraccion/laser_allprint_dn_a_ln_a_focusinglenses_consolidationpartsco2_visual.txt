For the same focal distance, there are dif ferent focal lens part numbers used between our old 3X20 / 3X30 series laser models and our current 3X40 series
CO2 laser units. T o reduce inventory and avoid confusion the part numbers are being consolidated. The following list is a part number cross reference for
dif ferent focusing lenses.
P ar t N u m b er f o r F o cu si n g L en ses f o r N e w L a s e r u n i t s - U s e d i n C o n f i g u r a t o r
F500 mm and F600 mm focusing modules still uses the AL-76258 and AL-76259 . These two lenses can only be used on a 30 and 60 watt laser
unit with SHC150 marking heads.
Both F63.5 mm and F85 mm variable focusing modules still use AL-69274 and AL-69263 part numbers respectively and can only be used on
SHC100 and SHC120 marking heads for both the 10 and 30 watt lasers.
The new Consolidated Part Number uses BBAR coated lenses where double V marks are engraved on the side of the lens.
Old part numbers will be deactivated when stock runs out. Use the new Consolidated Part Numbers when ordering spares.
