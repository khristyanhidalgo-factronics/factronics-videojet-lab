Exchange of the Q-Switch DN-A

1 Exchange of the Q-Switch
The Q-switch must be exchanged1.if it does not lock (the laser is not switched off during jumps) although the following condi-
tions are met:- the Q-switch is adjusted.
- the RF power supply is provided with 10 V.- the Q-switch is connected to the RF power supply via the BNC cable.
2.if the error message »E24201: Overtemperature of Q-switch« is displayed although the fol-lowing conditions are met:
- the cooling water is not too warm.- the cooling water gets to the Q-switch.
- the Q-switch is not clogged.
For exchanging the Q-switch the following is requiredhex key 2.5 mm
of the accessory kit:hex key 4 mm (66918)
protection caps (65550)
