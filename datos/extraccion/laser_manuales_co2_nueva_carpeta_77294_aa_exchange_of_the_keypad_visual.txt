1.2 Removal of the Keypad
1.Carefully disconnect the key switch connector and the keypad connector.
2.Remove the nut from the grounding bolt. Keep it in a safe place for mounting the new keypad.
3.Lift off the grounding flap of the keypad from the grounding bolt.
4.Remove the toothed disk from the grounding bolt. Keep it in a safe place for mounting thenew keypad.
5.Carefully pull off the flat cable from the adhesive tape (the tape must remain in place, it isrequired for fixing the new flat cable).

1.3 Mounting a new Keypad
NoteMake sure that the cover is not scratched.
1.Remove the foil from the new keypad.
2.Carefully insert the flat cable and the grounding flap through the openings.
3.Position the keypad so that all openings are sealed.
NoteMake sure that the grounding flap does not touch the edge of the opening toavoid shearing.
4.Press the keypad onto the cover.
5.Place the toothed disk onto the grounding bolt.
6.Put the grounding flap onto the grounding bolt.
7.Connect the key switch connector and the keypad connector.
8.Mount the keypad cable to the grounding bolt using the nut.
9.Close the cover.
