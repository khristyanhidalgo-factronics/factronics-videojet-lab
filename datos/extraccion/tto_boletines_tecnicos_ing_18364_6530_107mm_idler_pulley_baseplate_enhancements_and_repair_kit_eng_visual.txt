TS23031785870 -  6530 107mm Printer Idler Pulley Gets Ripped Off, Causing
Irreparable Damage to Baseplate Threads, Requiring Printer Replacement
Global TTO support has tracked numerous instances of 6530 107mm units exhibiting an issue in
which the idler pulley shaft breaks free from its retaining slot. When this occurs, it effectively
ruins the threads on the baseplate that secure the Idler Pulley shaft. As the baseplate is not a
replaceable item, this has required replacement of the printer.
Videojet is now introducing both a long- term and a field repair solution to address this issue.
Long- Term Solution
Videojet Engineering has developed a change to the 6530 107mm baseplate that will accom -
modate a countersunk screw. The countersunk screw enters into and secures the idler pulley
shaft. Rigorous internal testing has found no issue with this solution. New printers will ship with
this new design as of serial number 2323618005TZH .
Figure 1 shows the old baseplate and Figure 2 the changed baseplate.
l
The holes on the old baseplate (Figure 1, red circles) were threaded to accommodate the
male idler pulley shaft being threaded in on the opposite side.
l
The holes on the new baseplate (Figure 2, green circles) have a countersink so that a coun -
tersunk screw can be screwed into the female end of the new idler pulley shaft, which sits on
the opposite side.

Field Repair Solution
A field repair kits is now available for existing printers exhibiting this issue.
l
The Field Repair kit employs the same principles as the design change in which a screw
secures the idler pulley shaft.
l
The Field Repair solution will not require any drilling.
l
Videojet TTO Engineering and Technical Support have tested this solution internally as well
as externally at a few customer sites and found no issue.
l
The Field Repair solution part number is 430226 .
l
There is a new cutout on the motor plate (Figure 3, blue circle). This is to allow for the M3 x 8
button head screw (pictured in Figure 3).
l
The the idler pulley shaft no longer has a threaded male end and is now female with
threaded inserts (Figure 3, yellow circle).
l
The kit includes instructions.
Figure 3:  Field repair kit contents.
Applies to 107mm Only
Both solutions apply only to the 107mm variant as it is the only model that has had this
issue.
Spare Idler Pulley Kit Changes
The 107mm spare Idler Pulley kit part number will remain the same (P/N 408678). However, it
will now include two shafts (male and female) and two baseplate securing screws (M3 x 8 coun -
tersunk and M3 x 8 button head).
This new spare idler pulley kit will be implemented in batches with a date of 23 - Aug - 2023 and
beyond printed on the box.

Corrective Action
If an installed 6530 107mm printer exhibits the issue described above, use the field repair solu -
tion (P/N 430226).
Actions during Idler Pulley replacements
If replacing the pulley and/or pulley shaft for required maintenance using the Spare Idler Pulley
Kit (P/N 408678), install as follows based on the state of the baseplate:
l
Printer has the old baseplate and the threads on the baseplate are still intact and
undamaged
Use the male pulley shaft.  No M3 x 8 screw is needed .
l
Printer has the old baseplate and has previously required the field repair solution
Use the female pulley shaft with the M3 x 8 button head screw .
l
Printer has the new baseplate
Use the female pulley shaft with the M3 x 8 countersunk screw .
