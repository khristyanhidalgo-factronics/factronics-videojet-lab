Working Distance (7230, 7330, 7320, 7220)
The Working Distance of a marking head is the optimal distance from the front of the marking head
(red emissions LED cover) to the product. This is typically where the best print quality is achieved and
prevents scratches to the lens that would occur if focal distance were used as a measurement.
The following figure shows where the working distance is measured on the marking heads.
