Exchange and Cleaning of BTU Mirror

1 Exchange and Cleaning of BTU Mirror
The mirror in the beam turning unit (BTU) has to be cleanedin case of too low output power, caused by contaminations on the mirror surface.
The mirror has to be exchangedin case of too low output power, caused by burned-in spots on the mirror surface.
The following is requiredof the accessory kit:
hex key 3 mm (00478)of the maintenance kit:
optics cleaning paper (66294)acetone (66955)
protective gloves (66890)
1.1 Dismounting of the BTU Mirror
ImportantIf the laser system comprises several beam turning units only one BTU at atime must be dismounted and it has to be adjusted directly after remounting.
Never dismount several BTUs simultaneously!
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be dead!1.Turn the key switch to position »0«.
Remove the key to ensure that the laser system cannot be switched on.
2.Wait until the message »Key switch open« is displayed on the touch screen.
3.Switch off main switch.
4.Disconnect the mains supply.

Exchange and Cleaning of BTU Mirror
Dismount the BTU mirror as follows:1.Loosen the fixing screws of the mirror carrier and remove the mirror carrier with the mirror.
2.Place the mirror carrier with the mirror facing upwards onto a clean surface.
3.Loosen the fixing screws of the sheet metal holding the mirror and remove it from the mirror.Leave the mirror in the carrier for the cleaning process.
