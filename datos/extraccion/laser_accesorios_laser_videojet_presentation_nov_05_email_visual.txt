AD180 & AD400 Purification Systems
• Cost of Ownership
• 3 Years warranty on units
• Superior finish IP56 Cabinet manufactured in 304 food
grade brushed stainless steel
• Advantage 2 stage primary filter
• 99.997% HEPA/Gas combined filter
• Filter and system condition light display and interface to
laser
• Dual Voltage for world wide use (AD180)

2 Stage Primary Filter
• 9m2 of media surface area
• <95% efficiency to 1 micron for optimum protection of HEPA filter
• Large particulate drops to bottom of sump through expansion
• Particulate that builds on filter media breaks away, extending filter life further
• All particulate that has fallen away is held safely within the filter box
• Safe Change seal supplied with each filter

Secondary Combined Filter
• <99.997% efficiency at 0.3 micron HEPA
• Impregnated Activate carbon (10Kgs)
• All BOFA filters are manufactured by specialised
filter companies to BOFA international’s
specification. This ensures the highest quality of
manufacturing and testing techniques.
• Filter test Certificates of conformance supplied on
request
• Vapour contaminates list

Filtered Cooling Air
• Provides 200cfm of clean filtered air to laser
• The filter has an efficiency rating of 90% @ 1 micron
• Push fit Hose assembly kit with adapters
• Filter change indication light
• Easily replaceable filters
