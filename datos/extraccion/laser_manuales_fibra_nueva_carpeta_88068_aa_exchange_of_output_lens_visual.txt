1.2 Mounting of New Output Lens
1.Remove the inside and outside caps of the new lens assembly.
2.Check the new lens assembly, clean if necessary.
3.Make sure the o-ring is in the correct position.

1.3 Position Verification
1.To check the correct position of the lens log in to TCS+/LinxVision software.
2.Select Pilot Laser from the global settings menu.
3.Select Focus Finder and press Start.
4.The pilot laser simulates marking a ring with a dot in the center.
5.If it is not positioned correctly (dot not in center): Open the key switch, remove the lens usinga 3mm Allen key and check the position of the o-ring.
6.Repeat the focus finder function.
