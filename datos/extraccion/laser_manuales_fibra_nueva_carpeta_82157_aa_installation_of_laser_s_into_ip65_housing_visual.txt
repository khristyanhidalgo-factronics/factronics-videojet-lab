1 Mounting the Laser System into the IP65 Housing
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be completely discon-nected!
If only one laser system is to be installed it must be installed at the bot-tom. Otherwise the housing can tilt.
1.Place the laser system to be installed close to the housing.
2.At the back of the housing: Activate the brakes of the housing.
3.Remove the 6 outer screws of the bottom drawer using a 4 mm Allen key and keep themsafe for later assembly.
