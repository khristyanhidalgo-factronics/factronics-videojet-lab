3.4 Safety Features
The cooling device is equipped with the following safety facilities:
High-Pressure switch (HP) The high-pressure switch protects the
refrigerant cycle against extremely high operating pressure. In the event of failure the HP-switch switches off the compressor after exceeding the preset limit value of pressure and
can be restarted again only when the pressure changed by the pre-set pressure difference.
3.5 Monitoring Facilities
The cooling device is equipped with the following monitoring facilities:
Temperature ControllerThe temperature control and regulation is carried out by the temperature controller. The latter controls the water
temperature as a function The temperature controller with digital display indicatiof the pre-set value. ng the water temperature is integrated on the terminal box.
Float Switch Two float switches are located in the tank and monitors the water level (warning and dry-run protection). This enables the
constancy of the water outlet temperature and the dry-run protection of the pump to be ensured. Water shortage is hand on by a signal to the LPC, when the water level falls below the minimum. The cooling remains in
operation. In case the water filling level drops further and pump(s) are switched off (dry-run protection). The re-activatithe minimum limit value is reached, the cooling and the on of the cooling device is only possible, when the water is
filled up to the MAX.-marking at the filling level display.
Winding Protection - CompressorDepending on construction the reciprocating compressors are equipped with a winding
protection. The winding protection at reciprocating compressors are designed as Klixon-type.
Compressor Winding Protection - Klixon The compressors are equipped with a bi-metal switch. In the event of an increase in motor current in conjunction with a
rise in winding temperature, the compressor is switched off by the bimetal switch. The bimetal switch is reset automatically after the windings have
cooled down
Thermal Contact - Fan MotorIn the event of an increase in motor current in conjunction with a rise in winding temperature, cooling operation is
switched off and signals the malfunction by means of a indication lamp in the control cabinet. The thermal contact is reset automatically after the windings have cooled down.

4. TRANSPORT / STORAGE
4.1 Transport The cooling device must be transported in upright position without water charge. The in-house transport is to be carried
out by fork lift or platform lift-truck. During the general transport, ambient temperatures of 0°C to 63°C are permissible.
4.1.1 Transport - Fork Lift or Platform Lift Truck
The transporting of the cooling device is to carry out on a pallet to avoid damages. The lifting speed must be kept low!
Lifting speedKeep the lifting speed to a minimum (v lift < 10 m/min, precision lifting, jerk-free lifting)
Depositing The Cooling DeviceTo prevent any distortions of the lower frame, the cooling device must be lowered onto a level surface.
Transport-Specific DetailsSee Technical Data and Dimensions
4.2 Storage
The cooling device is to be stored on a level surface or supported on additional traverse beams placed underneath the hoist-attaching points, in dry, freeze-proof rooms permissible temperature 0°C - 63°C.
Storage at ambient temperature below 0°C requires special measures to be taken so as to avoid frost damages (Consult manufacturer).

5. INSTALLATION
The cooling device is to be installed as plug-in box. Any attachment to the foundation as well as intermediate spacers or vibration absorbing mounts are not required.
In the case of open water system, the cooling device must be installed on the same level as the customer. To ensure trouble-free operation of the cooling device as well as to provide adequate access for the performance of
maintenance and repair work, the minimum clearances must be observed during installation.
In case of different installation conditions, consult the manufacturer.
Care should be taken to ensure that the noise level of the refrigeration unit is within limits !
The room size for the installation of the refrigeration unit should be selected such that a clear suction and
blow-out of cooling air is ensured.
Make also sure that sufficient air change is provided for heat removal.
Minimum distances should be complied with for clear suction and blow-out and for servicing work!
The room should have a minimum size with respect to the existing refrigerant capacity of the refrigeration unit.
It is thus ensured that in case of damage a refrigerant leakage, and consequently an oxygen displacement,
would have no harmful effect on persons.

3.4 Safety Features
The cooling device is equipped with the following safety facilities:
High-Pressure switch (HP) The high-pressure switch protects the
refrigerant cycle against extremely high operating pressure. In the event of failure the HP-switch switches off the compressor after exceeding the preset limit value of pressure and
can be restarted again only when the pressure changed by the pre-set pressure difference.
3.5 Monitoring Facilities
The cooling device is equipped with the following monitoring facilities:
Temperature ControllerThe temperature control and regulation is carried out by the temperature controller. The latter controls the water
temperature as a function The temperature controller with digital display indicatiof the pre-set value. ng the water temperature is integrated on the terminal box.
Float Switch Two float switches are located in the tank and monitors the water level (warning and dry-run protection). This enables the
constancy of the water outlet temperature and the dry-run protection of the pump to be ensured. Water shortage is hand on by a signal to the LPC, when the water level falls below the minimum. The cooling remains in
operation. In case the water filling level drops further and pump(s) are switched off (dry-run protection). The re-activatithe minimum limit value is reached, the cooling and the on of the cooling device is only possible, when the water is
filled up to the MAX.-marking at the filling level display.
Winding Protection - CompressorDepending on construction the reciprocating compressors are equipped with a winding
protection. The winding protection at reciprocating compressors are designed as Klixon-type.
Compressor Winding Protection - Klixon The compressors are equipped with a bi-metal switch. In the event of an increase in motor current in conjunction with a
rise in winding temperature, the compressor is switched off by the bimetal switch. The bimetal switch is reset automatically after the windings have
cooled down
Thermal Contact - Fan MotorIn the event of an increase in motor current in conjunction with a rise in winding temperature, cooling operation is
switched off and signals the malfunction by means of a indication lamp in the control cabinet. The thermal contact is reset automatically after the windings have cooled down.

5. INSTALLATION
The cooling device is to be installed as plug-in box. Any attachment to the foundation as well as intermediate spacers or vibration absorbing mounts are not required.
In the case of open water system, the cooling device must be installed on the same level as the customer. To ensure trouble-free operation of the cooling device as well as to provide adequate access for the performance of
maintenance and repair work, the minimum clearances must be observed during installation.
In case of different installation conditions, consult the manufacturer.
Care should be taken to ensure that the noise level of the refrigeration unit is within limits !
The room size for the installation of the refrigeration unit should be selected such that a clear suction and
blow-out of cooling air is ensured.
Make also sure that sufficient air change is provided for heat removal.
Minimum distances should be complied with for clear suction and blow-out and for servicing work!
The room should have a minimum size with respect to the existing refrigerant capacity of the refrigeration unit.
It is thus ensured that in case of damage a refrigerant leakage, and consequently an oxygen displacement,
would have no harmful effect on persons.

The differential portion dampens temperature changes.
If lead time is set for long, damping is strong. At too long lead time, however, the system may tend
to vibrate. At setting 0 the values are ineffective. It is therefore possible to realise a pure PI or PD
regulation.

contacts. For very fast control ways with the respective high switching frequency a voltage output

3.4 Safety Features
The cooling device is equipped with the following safety facilities:
High-Pressure switch (HP) The high-pressure switch protects the
refrigerant cycle against extremely high operating pressure. In the event of failure the HP-switch switches off the compressor after exceeding the preset limit value of pressure and
can be restarted again only when the pressure changed by the pre-set pressure difference.
3.5 Monitoring Facilities
The cooling device is equipped with the following monitoring facilities:
Temperature ControllerThe temperature control and regulation is carried out by the temperature controller. The latter controls the water
temperature as a function The temperature controller with digital display indicatiof the pre-set value. ng the water temperature is integrated on the terminal box.
Float Switch Two float switches are located in the tank and monitors the water level (warning and dry-run protection). This enables the
constancy of the water outlet temperature and the dry-run protection of the pump to be ensured. Water shortage is hand on by a signal to the LPC, when the water level falls below the minimum. The cooling remains in
operation. In case the water filling level drops further and pump(s) are switched off (dry-run protection). The re-activatithe minimum limit value is reached, the cooling and the on of the cooling device is only possible, when the water is
filled up to the MAX.-marking at the filling level display.
Winding Protection - CompressorDepending on construction the reciprocating compressors are equipped with a winding
protection. The winding protection at reciprocating compressors are designed as Klixon-type.
Compressor Winding Protection - Klixon The compressors are equipped with a bi-metal switch. In the event of an increase in motor current in conjunction with a
rise in winding temperature, the compressor is switched off by the bimetal switch. The bimetal switch is reset automatically after the windings have
cooled down
Thermal Contact - Fan MotorIn the event of an increase in motor current in conjunction with a rise in winding temperature, cooling operation is
switched off and signals the malfunction by means of a indication lamp in the control cabinet. The thermal contact is reset automatically after the windings have cooled down.

5. INSTALLATION
The cooling device is to be installed as plug-in box. Any attachment to the foundation as well as intermediate spacers or vibration absorbing mounts are not required.
In the case of open water system, the cooling device must be installed on the same level as the customer. To ensure trouble-free operation of the cooling device as well as to provide adequate access for the performance of
maintenance and repair work, the minimum clearances must be observed during installation.
In case of different installation conditions, consult the manufacturer.
Care should be taken to ensure that the noise level of the refrigeration unit is within limits !
The room size for the installation of the refrigeration unit should be selected such that a clear suction and
blow-out of cooling air is ensured.
Make also sure that sufficient air change is provided for heat removal.
Minimum distances should be complied with for clear suction and blow-out and for servicing work!
The room should have a minimum size with respect to the existing refrigerant capacity of the refrigeration unit.
It is thus ensured that in case of damage a refrigerant leakage, and consequently an oxygen displacement,
would have no harmful effect on persons.

C82: Proportional band at PID regulation

C83: Reset time at PID regulation (I - portion)
C84: Lead time at PID regulation (D - portion)
The proportional con troller as such has a remaining deviation of the actual value from the setpoint.
The integral portion provides for a complete compensation of this offset.
The reset time is a measure for the period of time needed to adjust a remaining temperature
deviation of the size of the proportional range.
If a small reset time is set, a fast post - adjustment will take place. At a too small reset time,
however, the system may tend to vibrate.
The differential portion dampens temperature changes.
If lead time is set for long, damping is strong. At too long lead time, however, the system may tend
to vibrate. At setting 0 the values are ineffective. It is therefore possible to realise a pure PI or PD
regulation.
C85: Cycle time at PID regulation
During the cycle time the c ontrol exit runs through one switching period, i.e. once switched out and
once switched on. The smaller the cycle time, the faster the regulation. By consequence, however,
there is also an increased switching frequency of the output, which can lead to rapi d wear of relay
contacts. For very fast control ways with the respective high switching frequency a voltage output
is therefore of advantage.

3.4 Safety Features
The cooling device is equipped with the following safety facilities:
High-Pressure switch (HP) The high-pressure switch protects the
refrigerant cycle against extremely high operating pressure. In the event of failure the HP-switch switches off the compressor after exceeding the preset limit value of pressure and
can be restarted again only when the pressure changed by the pre-set pressure difference.
3.5 Monitoring Facilities
The cooling device is equipped with the following monitoring facilities:
Temperature ControllerThe temperature control and regulation is carried out by the temperature controller. The latter controls the water
temperature as a function The temperature controller with digital display indicatiof the pre-set value. ng the water temperature is integrated on the terminal box.
Float Switch Two float switches are located in the tank and monitors the water level (warning and dry-run protection). This enables the
constancy of the water outlet temperature and the dry-run protection of the pump to be ensured. Water shortage is hand on by a signal to the LPC, when the water level falls below the minimum. The cooling remains in
operation. In case the water filling level drops further and pump(s) are switched off (dry-run protection). The re-activatithe minimum limit value is reached, the cooling and the on of the cooling device is only possible, when the water is
filled up to the MAX.-marking at the filling level display.
Winding Protection - CompressorDepending on construction the reciprocating compressors are equipped with a winding
protection. The winding protection at reciprocating compressors are designed as Klixon-type.
Compressor Winding Protection - Klixon The compressors are equipped with a bi-metal switch. In the event of an increase in motor current in conjunction with a
rise in winding temperature, the compressor is switched off by the bimetal switch. The bimetal switch is reset automatically after the windings have
cooled down
Thermal Contact - Fan MotorIn the event of an increase in motor current in conjunction with a rise in winding temperature, cooling operation is
switched off and signals the malfunction by means of a indication lamp in the control cabinet. The thermal contact is reset automatically after the windings have cooled down.

5. INSTALLATION
The cooling device is to be installed as plug-in box. Any attachment to the foundation as well as intermediate spacers or vibration absorbing mounts are not required.
In the case of open water system, the cooling device must be installed on the same level as the customer. To ensure trouble-free operation of the cooling device as well as to provide adequate access for the performance of
maintenance and repair work, the minimum clearances must be observed during installation.
In case of different installation conditions, consult the manufacturer.
Care should be taken to ensure that the noise level of the refrigeration unit is within limits !
The room size for the installation of the refrigeration unit should be selected such that a clear suction and
blow-out of cooling air is ensured.
Make also sure that sufficient air change is provided for heat removal.
Minimum distances should be complied with for clear suction and blow-out and for servicing work!
The room should have a minimum size with respect to the existing refrigerant capacity of the refrigeration unit.
It is thus ensured that in case of damage a refrigerant leakage, and consequently an oxygen displacement,
would have no harmful effect on persons.
