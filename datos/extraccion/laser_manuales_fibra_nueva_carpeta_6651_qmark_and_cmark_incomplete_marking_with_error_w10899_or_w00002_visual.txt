Incomplete Marking with Error W10899 or W00002 Caused
by Leakage Protection Feature
Lasers with the Leakage Protection feature enabled may fault on incomplete or unmarked mes -
sages when some events increase the amount of time between trigger signals and marking or
interrupt marking entirely.
Leakage Protection is intended to prevent power leaks on aging beam sources.
Leakage Protection prevents damage to conveyors or product by setting the shutter to a closed
position after 10 seconds (QMark) or reducing the laser intensity to 0% after 500 ms (CMark).
The time required for movement to and from the shutter closed galvo position or power charge
delay to return to normal laser intensity creates an additional variable that can result in incom -
plete marks when product speed varies or is interrupted. The higher the throughput of an applic -
ation, the more likely leakage protection is to negatively impact marking speed.
Symptoms
Encoder Applications
l Faulty marking while marking with the activated encoder.
l Warning W10899: Marking incomplete, Product movement too slow.
Fixed Speed Applications
l Increased marking time can lead to faulty marking.
l Warning W00002: Marking incomplete, Product movement too fast.
l Error E00105: Marking aborted, Product movement too fast.

Corrective Actions (QMark)
Encoding Applications
After switching off the conveyor for more than 9 seconds marking must be stopped if unmarked
products are in the trigger queue.
1. Make sure that the product movement is no less than 10 mm/s for more than 9 seconds .
2. If the conveyor is stopped for more than 9 seconds make sure that no trigger signal is
released.
3. Deactivate Leakage protection in the Laser Web Interface.
Fixed Speed Applications
1. Connect to the laser web interface.
2. Deactivate Leakage protection.
3. Increase the value for Start distance in the software under Product Registration.
Corrective Actions (CMark)
Encoding Applications
After switching off the conveyor, the marking must be stopped if unmarked products are in the
trigger queue.
1. Make sure that the product movement is not less than 10 mm/s for more than 500 ms .
2. Deactivate Leakage protection in the Laser Web Interface.
Fixed Speed Applications
1. Connect to the laser web interface.
2. Deactivate Leakage protection.
Note: Stop Marking Prior to Line Stops
Stop marking prior to long pauses in throughput (e.g. line stops) to prevent the laser
remaining in "Wait for Trigger", "Wait for Trigger delay" or "Marking" states.
