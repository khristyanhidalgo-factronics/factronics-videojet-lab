Height Adjustment Table
10.1 How to Use the Height Adjustment Table
1.Measure the height of the conveyor belt.
NoteFor pre-selection a range of 5mm is recommended.
2.Measure the product height.
3.Check the table. Find the measured heights.
Example: Measured height is 930mm (5), product height is 150mm
930mm can be found in the column of leg hole 1, 2, and 3.
Leg hole 3 is too high, the distance between minimum focus and product is too high (no Z axis value at the product height).
Leg hole 2 is possible, but a matching focus miof the Z axis, therefore it is not recommended.ght only be possible with the lowest position
Leg hole 1 is the best solution, Z axis can be positioned in the middle of the adjustment range.
