Trigger Settings
Trigger Mode
‘External Rising’
-- Fires off rising edge of pulse from trigger source
‘External Falling’
-- Fires off falling edge of pulse from trigger source
‘Internal Once’
-- Used for static print
‘Internal Continuous’
-- Will fire continuously
-- Care is necessary when selecting this option as
dangerous situations may occur

• Use message created in previous lab (shown below)
• Change to product movement (Fixed speed)
• Create a new Line setup (name)
-- Set up ‘wizard’
• Position and Orientation
-- Direction of Line Movement
• Line Speed
-- Enter Fixed Speed
• Metres / min
• Trigger
-- Start Distance
• Should be not less than 50% of the marking area of the lens
in that axis
• This is the physical distance of the Product Sensor to the
laser
• May need to be longer to allow mirrors time to reach start
position
-- Blocking Distance – 90% of Product pitch
