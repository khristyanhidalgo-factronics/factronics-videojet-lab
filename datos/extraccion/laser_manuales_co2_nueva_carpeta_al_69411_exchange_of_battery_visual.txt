1 Exchange of the Battery
The following tool is required for exchanging the battery:
Double offset screw driver 6-edged 4 mm
1.1 Dismounting
Supply Unit Type 1
1.Loosen both screws of the housing cover by a half turn and remove the cover.
Caution1.Turn the key-operated switch in the position »0«.Remove the key, in order to ensure that the laser system cannot be
switched-on.
2.Start the main switch so that no data are lost on the CPU board.
Supply Unit Type 2 NoteA battery is mounted on the CPU PCB board to provide auxiliary power forthe clock and other functions when the system is shut down.
If this battery is replaced with an incorrect type, a hazardous condition couldresult.
WarningDanger of explosion exists if battery is incorrectly replaced.
Replace only with the same or equivalent type.
Dispose of used batteries according to the battery manufacturer'sinstructions.
