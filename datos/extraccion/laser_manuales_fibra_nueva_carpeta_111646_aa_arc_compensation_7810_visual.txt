1 Configuration of Product Registration for Arc Compensation
There are two possibilities to configure the rotary table (arc compensation)
1.Configuration via Web-Interface (recommended)
2.Configuration via Text-Editor and WinSCP (not recommended and not described in this man-ual)
1.1 Configuration via Web-Interface
Preconditions
Smart Graph: Version 8.2.3T5 or later
Web-Interface: Version 1.0.2T2 or later
CMark: Version 1.0.2T5 or later
Procedure
NoteCreate a special product registration in Smart Graph to protect the defaults.
1.Start Web-Interface.
2.Enter Laser IP @port 8080 in a browser i.e. http://192.168.1.1:8080
3.Log in (at least) as Service.
4.Navigate to Arc-Compensation Config.

The following values can be entered for the rotary table configuration:
NoteAs the vector between marking field center and rotational axis are both vir-tual axes it is helpful to measure these x- and y-values in the 3D-model of the
production line.
X-Offset: X value in mm between rotation center of the rotary table and thecenter of the marking field (distance marked green).
Y-Offset:Y value in mm between rotation center of the rotary table and thecenter of the marking field (distance marked violet).
Ratio(Table : Encoder) -> 1:Depends on the position of the encoder (i.e. encoder at the rota-tional axis, the value would be 1)
Pulses per Encoder Turn: Resolution of the encoder.
The diameter of the encoder and the diameter of the rotary tablecan be used to configure a transformation ratio between encoder
and rotary table.
The rotation direction can be changed by the sign of the Pulses-PerEncoderTurn value. Positive values mean counter clockwise
rotations. Negative values mean clockwise rotations.
