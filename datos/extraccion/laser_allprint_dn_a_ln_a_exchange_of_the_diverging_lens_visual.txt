Exchange of the Diverging Lens

1 Exchange of the Diverging Lens
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be dead!
1.Open the housing cover of the laser head.
2.Remove the beam protection tube between output coupler and beam expander.
3.Loosen the adapter ring of the diverging lens and remove the diverging lens.
4.Insert the new diverging lens into the beam expander.Make sure that the reference plane is facing upwards.
5.Tighten the adapter ring.
6.Place the beam protection tube between output coupler and beam expander.
7.Close the housing cover of the laser head.
