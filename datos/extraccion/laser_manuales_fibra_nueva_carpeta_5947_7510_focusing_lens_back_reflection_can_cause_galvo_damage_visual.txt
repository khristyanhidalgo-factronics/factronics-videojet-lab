7510 Focusing Lens Back Reflection Can Cause Galvo Damage

Overview
Overview
Videojet Engineering has identified a potential problem on VJ7510 fiber laser units in which back
reflection from focusing lenses can damage the galvos.
Affected units include VJ7510 systems up to serial number Y1504/023 with CMark software
1.2.0.7 or below.
VJ7510 laser units with F100 focusing lens are most at risk.
New CMark 1.2.0.8 and compatible SmartGraph 8.5.3.6.10 software is available to help prevent this
issue.
Installation of a new focusing lens and/or adaptor ring may be required..
Working Distance/Marking Distance and available marking field have changed on some focusing
lenses.
Products
LASER.7510; LASER.SOFTWARE.CMARK
Affected
Symptoms
Symptoms
The only known symptom of this issue is otherwise early failure of galvos.
Corrective
Action

Corrective Action
In addition to the new software, some hardware may need to be replaced to prevent back reflection.
1. Update the CMark software to version 1.2.0.8.
2. Replace/Install lens and/or adapter as necessary.
