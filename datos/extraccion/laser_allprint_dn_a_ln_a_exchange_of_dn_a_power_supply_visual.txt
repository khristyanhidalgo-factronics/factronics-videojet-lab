Exchange of the DN-A Power Supply Modules

1 Exchange of the DN-A Power Supply Modules
The following is required for exchanging the power supply modules:hex key 2.5 mm
offset hex key 5 mmterminal (63461)
1.1 Preparation
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be dead!1.Turn the key switch to position »0«.
Remove the key to ensure that the laser system cannot be switched on.
2.Wait until the message »Key switch open« is displayed on the touch screen.
3.Switch off main switch.
4.Disconnect the mains supply.
1.Remove the front panel of the PCM housing.
2.Loosen the safety interlock (2 turns) and remove the lateral fixing screw of the supply unit.
3.Pull the supply unit out of the PCM.

Exchange of the DN-A Power Supply Modules
1.2 Exchange of the 500 W/27 V Power Supply Modules
1.Remove the eight fixing screws of the two modules from the bottom of the supply unit.
2.Lift the modules, remove the covers, disconnect the lines and remove the modules.
3.Insert the new modules into the supply unit, connect all lines and mount the covers.
4.Tighten the fixing screws of the modules at the bottom of the supply unit.
5.Push the supply unit into the PCM, tighten the lateral fixing screw and lock the supply unit.
6.Mount the front panel of the PCM.

1.3 Exchange of the Diode Power Supply Module
DangerWear a grounding wrist strap during the following steps.
1.Remove the two fixing screws of the diode power supply from the bottom of the supply unit.
2.Disconnect the 24V connector (X 104) and the connector of the laser power supply (X 106).
3.Loosen and remove the connections of the diode power supply.
.
ImportantThe pump chamber connections must be short-circuited using the terminal.
4.Remove the diode power supply module.
5.Insert the new diode power supply into the supply unit, connect the two connectors (X104and X106) and the six connections accordingly.
6.Tighten the screws of the diode power supply module at the bottom of the supply module.
7.Push the supply unit into the PCM, tighten the lateral fixing screw and lock the supply unit.
8.Mount the front panel of the PCM.
