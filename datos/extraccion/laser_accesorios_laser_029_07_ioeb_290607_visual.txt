two new available functions:

Thanks to this important add on on the IOEB,

2: external job selection via ioeB possible

• jobs can be selected fully automatic via PLC > that saves working hour expenses
• the customer‘s flexibility with regards to the integration of our laser markers in fully automatic lines is
 increased
