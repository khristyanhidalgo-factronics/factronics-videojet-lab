U s i n g T h e T T O P r i n t e r A l i g n m e n t T o o l ( 4 0 8 6 8 8 )
The TT O alignment tool will hello you ensure the printer and its components are aligned and mounted
square/parallel onto the printer bracket. It can be used on the 32mm, 53mm, and 107mmm printer variants. The
premise is the same for all three variants with the only dif ference being the 107mm does not have a lightbox.
For the 32mm and 53mm versions, perform the following procedures in order:
1. Align the Lightbox (32mm and 53mm V ersion)
2. Align the Peel Roller to the Aligned Lightbox (32mm and 53mm V ersion)
3. Align the Printer to the Print Roller
4. Align the Peel Roller to the Print Roller
For the 107mm version, perform the following procedures in order:
1. Align the Peel Roller (107mm V ersion)
2. Align the Printer to the Print Roller
3. Align the Peel Roller to the Print Roller
A l i g n i n g t h e L i g h t b o x ( 32m m an d 53m m V e r s i o n )
1. Place the printer alignment tool against the printer base plate and the lightbox. Y ou can use either of the
configurations below as the important part is the tool is perfectly square to the baseplate and lightbox.

2. Ensure the lightbox is aligned with the printer alignment tool so that is not askew and that it is parallel with
the tool.
If the lightbox is not aligned:
1. Loosen the two screws.
2. Align the lightbox to the tool and re-tighten the two screws.
A l i g n i n g t h e P eel R o l l er t o t h e A l i g n e d L i g h t b o x ( 3 2 m m a n d 5 3 m m V e r s i o n )
After it has been verified the lightbox is aligned, we can now use the lightbox as a reference to ensure the peel roller
is aligned.
1. Push the carriage forward so that the peel roller is against the lightbox.
2. V erify the peel roller is parallel to the lightbox. If it is not, replace the peel roller or peel arm assembly .
A l i g n i n g t h e P eel R o l l er ( 107m m V er s i o n )
1. Place the printer alignment tool against the peel roller and the printer base plate in either of the
configurations below .as the important part is the tool is perfectly square to the baseplate and the peel
roller .

A l i g n i n g t h e P r i n t er t o t h e P r i n t R o l l er
1. After it has been verified the peel roller is aligned, mount the printer onto the printer bracket and place the
printer alignment tool over the peel roller .
2. With the printer OFF , push the carriage forward or backward until the printer alignment tool comes in
contact with the the printer roller .
3. V erify there is no gap between the alignment tool and the print roller . If a gap exists, loosen the 4 printer
mounting screws, realign the printer , and then re-check. Below is a picture depicting a printer that is
aligned with the print roller and one that is not.

A l i g n i n g t h e P eel R o l l er t o t h e P r i n t R o l l e r
1. With the power OFF , move the carriage so that the peel roller is over the center of the print roller .
2. Place the printer alignment tool between the peel roller and the print roller .

3. Use the provided shims to shim the printer accordingly so that the peel roller falls in between the specified
marks on the alignment tool as this will help ensure a 2mm printhead to platen distance is achieved when
the printer is ready to print onto the print roller . Below is a picture depicting the printer sensing a 2mm
printhead to platen distance after the printer was shimmed so the peel roller lands between the two
alignment marks.
*NOTE: Do not power on the printer until all of the steps in this document have been carried out
4. Rotate the tool to ensure the gap on the front and back of the peel roller are equal to the print roller . If not,
level the printer from front to back using the provided shims.
5. Once it is verified the printer and all of its components are aligned, the printer can be powered on and the
necessary printer configuration can take place.
