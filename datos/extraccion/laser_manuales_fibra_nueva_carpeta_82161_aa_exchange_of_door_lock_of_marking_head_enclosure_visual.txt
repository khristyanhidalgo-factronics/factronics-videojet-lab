AL-82161Service Part Installation Instruction
Exchange of Door Lock of Marking Head Enclosure
1 Exchange of Door Lock of Marking Head Enclosure
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be completely discon-nected!
1.Open the door.
2.Loosen the screw of the lock.
3.Remove the cam.
4.Loosen the nut.
5.Remove the lock base from the door.
6.Insert the new base from outside.
7.On the inside mount the nut.When tightening the nut make sure that the position of the cam corresponds to the alignment
of the keyhole (closed lock in the figure below).
8.Mount the cam.
9.Tighten the screw.
10.Close the door.
