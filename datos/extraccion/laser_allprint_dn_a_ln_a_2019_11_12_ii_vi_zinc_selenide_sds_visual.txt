4. First Aid Measures
4.1 Description of first aid measures
Inhalation If adverse effects occur, remove to uncontaminated area. Give
artificial respiration if not breathing. If breathing is difficult, oxygen should be administered by qualified personnel. Get immediate
medical attention.
Skin Contact Wash with soap and water.
Eye Contact Wash eyes immediately with large amounts of water or normal saline,
occasionally lifting upper and lower lids, until no evidence of chemical
remains. Get medical attention if irritation persists.
