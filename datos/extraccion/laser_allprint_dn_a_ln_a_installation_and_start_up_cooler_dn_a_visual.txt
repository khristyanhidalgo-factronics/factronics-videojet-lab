Installation and Start-Up DN-A

1 Start-up of the DN Cooling Unit
AttentionBefore the first start-up of the laser system and after any mainte-nance works on the cooling system the cooling system must be bled
carefully.The laser cooling circuit must be filled exclusively with a solution of
Algizid and distilled water.Make sure that no dirt gets to the connections of the cooling hoses.
Observe the safety data sheet "Chloramine-T Hydrate" in chapter "Appendix"!
For the start-up the following is required of the accessory kit:
funnel (66252)connecting piece (65287)
of the maintenance kit:Algizid (66590, Chloramine-T Hydrate)
1.Dissolve the contents of the small glass (Algizid) in 5 liters distilled water or use the pre-pared mixture provided by ALLTEC.
2.Remove the front and the back panel of the PCM housing.
3.Pass the cooling hoses of the marking unit under the cooling unit and connect the coolinghoses to the connections for initial water and return water.
Observe the color-code of the hoses (initial water: blue, return water: red)!
4.Mount the back panel and check the traction relief of the hoses.
5.Check whether a white filter element (5 µm) is in the filter and whether the hoses of the liquidcooler are connected to the cooling unit.
6.Remove the stopper from the fill pipe by pressing the guard ring down while pulling out thestopper.
7.Connect the funnel and fill the tank up to the and Algizid.maximum with the solution of distilled water
8.Open the filter housing for a short time to remove air from the pump.

Attention1.Connect the mains connector.

3.Wait until the message »Key switch open« is displayed on the touch screen.
4.The key switch remains in position »0«.Remove the key in order to ensure that the laser system cannot be switched
on.
5.Change the touch screen from display »Status« to display »System«.
6.Press button »Bleeding« and make sure that no water leaks from the laser system.
AttentionIf after 5 seconds no water is pumped into the filter housing press again but-ton »Bleeding« to stop the bleeding process and open the filter housing for a
short time.

up to the maximum.
