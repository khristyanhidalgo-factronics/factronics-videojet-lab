1.2 Mounting
1.Insert the pilot laser into the Galvo block and fasten it with the help of the fixing screw.Make sure that the recess of the socket of pilot laser adapter is located below.
2.Insert the new Galvo block into the marking head. Connect the pilot laser connector X122 with the connector X123.
3.Mount the Galvo block by tightening the fixing screws.
4.Connect both scanner connectors.The X scanner cable is marked with a blue shrink-on hose.
5.Connect the connectors of the LED.Make sure that the red cable of the LED is connected to the connector of the 24 V supply.
6.Close the housing cover and tighten the fixing screws.
7.Fasten the focusing optics on the marking head.
