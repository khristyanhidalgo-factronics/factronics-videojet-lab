1 Exchange of the Marking Head for LC100/3120
The following is required for exchanging the marking head:
offset hex key 3 mm and 2.5 mm
hex key 1.5 mm
spanner 24 mm (rectangular beam path)
spanner 22 mm (straight beam path)
1.1 Dismounting (rectangular beam path)
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be dead!
1.Turn the key-operated switch in the position »0«.Remove the key, in order to ensure that the laser system cannot be
switched-on.
2.Save the database in Smart Graph-Software, user interface »File«. For a detailed description see the manual of Smart Graph-Software.
3.Turn-off the main switch.
4.Pull the mains plug.
1.Loosen the fixing screws of the housing cover of the marking head and open the cover untilyou can disconnect the connectors of the red LED.
