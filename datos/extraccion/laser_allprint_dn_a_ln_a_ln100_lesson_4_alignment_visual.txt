Lesson 4 – Installation
Introduction
❑ This lesson will provide the participants with hands - on experience in the
Alignment of the LN100/DN50 YAG printer. The participants will
connect all pertinent accessories to the printers.
❑ Warning: all participants to use safety glasses
❑ Warning: open beam .
❑ Warning: Keep hands clear of beam during alignment adjustment .
❑ Caution: the participants are not to connect or disconnect any
cables or equipment to and from the printer unless power is off.
❑ Caution: door must be close and outside light on prior to printing.
Objectives
❑ Demonstrate the ability to safely Align a laser
❑ Demonstrate the ability to properly measure the output power .
❑ Demonstrate the ability to use the service monitor software .
❑ Demonstrate the ability to adjust the beam expander .
❑ Demonstrate the ability to the q - switch and aperture.
