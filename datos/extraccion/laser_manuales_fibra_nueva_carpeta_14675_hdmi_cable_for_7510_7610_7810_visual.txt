Fiber lasers (7510/7610) and UV 7810 have a dif ferent part number for the TU430 (AL-78185) than
the part number for CO2 TU430 (AL-78550). The cable of TU430 for fiber/UV is stripped in a specific
place to be used to create a ground inside the laser and the connector housing removed.
There is no dedicated port on the outside of the 7510, 7610, 7810 laser . The cable must be fed
through the cable gland plate and connected to the Chameleon board.
Use the following procedure if CO2 TU430 is inadvertently ordered or if the cable on the TU430 for
7510/7610/7810 must be replaced.
1. Remove the housing surrounding the HDMI connector on cable by unscrewing the black
plastic cover from the HDMI cable connector . The metal sleeve should slide of f.
2. Cut 10mm of the cover of f the harness approximately 520 mm back from the HDMI
connector . This is where you will ground the harness to the frame.

3. Connect the stripped section of the cable to the grounding bar (T -shaped metal brackets)
with a cable tie securely .
