Installation and Start-Up LN-A

1 Start-up of Water/Water Cooler with non-DI Compressor Chiller or Municipal Water
For the start-up the following is required:filter key (66120, located on the bottom plate of the PCM housing)
of the accessory kit:funnel (66252)
connecting piece (65287)of the maintenance kit:
DI resin (66599)
1.1 Connection of the internal cooling circuit
AttentionBefore the first start-up of the laser system and after any mainte-nance works on the cooling system the cooling system must be bled
carefully.The laser cooling circuit must be filled exclusively with distilled water
without any additives.Make sure that no dirt gets to the connections of the cooling hoses.
1.Remove the front and the back panel of the PCM housing.
2.Connect the cooling water hoses to the marking unit. Observe the color-code of the hoses (initial water: blue, return water: red)!
3.Check the connection for the marking unit at the water/water cooler.
4.Remove the stopper from the fill pipe by pressing the guard ring down while pulling out thestopper.
5.Connect the funnel and fill the tank with distilled water up to the maximum.
6.Check whether the filter (1µm) is in the left filter cup.
7.Open the right filter cup using the filter key and fill it with 400ml DI resin.
ImportantMake sure that no DI resin gets on the sealing.Pull the pressure plate down to the white filter to make contact between
pressure plate and DI resin.
8.Mount the right filter cup using the filter key.

1.3 Bleeding of the Laser System
Attention1.Connect the mains connector.
2.Switch on the main switch.
3.Wait until the message »Key switch open« is displayed on the touch screen.
4.The key switch remains in position »0«.Remove the key in order to ensure that the laser system cannot be switched
on.
5.Change the touch screen from display »Status« to display »System«.
6.Press button »Bleeding« and make sure that no water leaks from the system.
AttentionIf after 5 seconds no water is pumped into the filter housing press again but-ton »Bleeding« to stop the bleeding process and open the filter housing for a
short time.
7.Check the water level in the expansion tank of the water/water cooler. Before the water levelsinks below the minimum press »Bleeding« to stop the bleeding process and refill the water
up to the maximum.
8.Repeat steps 6 to 7 until the water level does not sink below the maximum.
9.Let the circulating pump run until there are no air bubbles in the left filter housing (approx.60seconds).
10.Mount the front panel of the PCM housing.
