ADDENDUM TO OPERATING INSTRUCTIONS
Pulldate Insert
The insert is added to a Message ( Template ) by creating an External Text insert and using the followi ng
command syntax in the default Text field :
PULL<offset><.day><rolltime><format>
Where,
• <offset> is the number of weeks (1 to 999) to offset the pulldate insert value. The value of the
insert is calculated by adding an offset in weeks to the followi ng pull day .
• <.day> is an optional parameter to specify the pull day of week on which to base the pulldate
insert value. Valid entries are ".B", ".M", ".T", ".W", ".R", ".F" and ".S" for Sunday through
Saturday, respectively. The default is Monday.
• < rolltime> is an optional parameter to specify the time offset from 00:00 on the Pull Day to begin
the week. The value is in the form (+/ - )HHMM .
• <format> is an optional parameter to specify a custom format. If this parameter is omitted, the
format will default to “ MMM DD YY ”
The following sub formats can be combined in any order with any punctuation or space characters to
create custom formats :
• MMM Where, MMM is the 3 character abbreviation of the month are J AN , F EB , M AR , A PR , M AY , J UN , J UL ,
A UG , S EP , O CT , N OV and D EC .
• MM Where, MM is the 2 character abbreviation of the month are JA , FE , MR , AL , MA , JN , JL , AU , SE , OC ,
NO and DE .
• NN Where, NN is the 2 digit month.
• DD Where, DD is the 2 digit day of the month.
• Y Where, Y is the 1 digit year.
• YY Where, YY is the 2 digit year.
• YYYY Where, YYYY is the 4 digit year.
