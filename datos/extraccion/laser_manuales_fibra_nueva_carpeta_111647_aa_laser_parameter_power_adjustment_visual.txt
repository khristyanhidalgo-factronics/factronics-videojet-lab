Laser Parameter - Power Adjustment
1 Laser Parameter - Power Adjustment
1.1 Overview
It is possible to set the following laser parameters (e.g. Smart Graph 8.5.3.6):
Marking Intensity
The marking intensity can be set between 0% and 100%. Physically, the current of the pumpdiodes of the Matrix laser is changed by this parameter. A change of the intensity results in a
power change delay of 70 s. The Matrix laser needs this time in order to change the current ofits pump diodes.
Pulse frequency
Repetition rate of the laser pulses, can be set between 10 kHz and 95 kHz.

Q-Switch width
The Q-Switch width is the time that the Q-Switch of the Matrix laser is open. During the time "1/f - Q-Switch width" the Q-switch is closed and energy for the laser pulse is stored within the
active material of the Matrix laser. The limitation of the maximum Q-Switch width of 0.95 * (1/f)ensures a minimum time for the energy storage. However, the shorter the time "1/f - Q-Switch
width", the less energy the laser pulse exhibits (see figure 1-1 below).

1.2 Power adjustment
For the laser marking Systems V.0020-uv and Videojet 7810 the UV-laser model Matrix 355-1-60 is implemented.
With the laser parameter intensity = 100 %, Pulse frequency = 60 kHz and a Q-Switch width =2 μs a laser power of ~ 1 W shall be achieved. If the frequency is changed to 20 kHz the
maximum optical output power of ~ 2.2 W shall be the result. In case the intensity is reducedand/or the Q-Switch width in increased (pulse frequency remains constant) the laser power will
decrease.
The UV laser output power can be adjusted by changing the intensity. However, a change ofthe parameter intensity always results in a power change delay of 70 s.
The laser power can also be modified without changing the intensity and therefore without thepower change delay. As indicated in figure 1-1 a shorter Q-Switch width leads to a higher pulse
energy and hence to a higher average output power in case the frequency remains constant.
