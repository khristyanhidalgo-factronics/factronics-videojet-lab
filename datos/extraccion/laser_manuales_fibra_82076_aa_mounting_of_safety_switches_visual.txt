1 Mounting of Safety Switches
1.1 Safety Switches of Beam Shield Tunnel
DangerBefore starting any work:
The laser system must be completely disconnected.
The conveyor must be at standstill.
NoteFor mounting of the safety switches use only M4x10 screws (AL-01102).
Do not use the screws provided by Schmersal. They are too long and maydamage the housing.
1.Open the service door of the beam shield tunnel.
2.Install the parts without connector to the doors.Remove the caps to access the screws.
3.Install the parts with connectors to the side panel of the beam shield tunnel.
4.Seal all screws with the caps.
5.Guide the wires upwards to the marking head enclosure and connect them to the safety swit-ches in the enclosure to form a daisy chain of connections, see Wiring Diagram on page5.
