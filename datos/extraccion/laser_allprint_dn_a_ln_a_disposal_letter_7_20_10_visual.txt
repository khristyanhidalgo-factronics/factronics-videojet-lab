You have asked for information on the proper disposal of your used CO
 laser optics.
The materials that these optics are made of 2 (GaAs); Germanium (Ge); Copper Zinc Selenide (ZnSe); Gallium Arsenide (Cu); Molybdenum (Mo); Silicon (Si); Zinc Sulfide
(ZnS); and Indium (In) are not considered hazardous material.
1. Dispose of all used CO2 laser optics (including Indium) in a licensed industrial waste facility.
2. If you do not have access to a licensed industrial waste facility, you can return the used optics to II-VI Infrared (Freight Prepaid), and we will dispose or recycle
the used optics (including Indium):
When disposing of used optics, II-VI Infrared uses only licensed industrial waste facilities and complies with all state and federal regulations.
To return used optics to II-VI Infrared, please:
 1. Call your Sales Representative at 724-352-1504 and ask for a Recycle Authorization number (DP#).
3. Return the material Freight Prepaid with the DP number on the address label.
