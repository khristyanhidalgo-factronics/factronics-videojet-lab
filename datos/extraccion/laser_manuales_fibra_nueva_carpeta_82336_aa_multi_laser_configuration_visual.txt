1.1 Adjustment for Beverage Can Application
1.For the beverage can application, restrict the available marking area to a minimum (notlarger than the can bottom width) whereby the laser system should still be able to complete
the marking at the required product speed. This is useful to ensure that the product is in better focus during the marking because of the
shape of the can bottom.
