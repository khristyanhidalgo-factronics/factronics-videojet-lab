Exchange of the Power Supply Modules

1 Exchange of the Power Supply Modules
The following is required for exchanging the power supply modules:hex key 3 mm (00478, accessory kit)
offset hex key 5 mm
1.1 Preparation
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be dead!1.Turn the key switch to position »0«.
Remove the key to ensure that the laser system cannot be switched on.
2.Wait until the message »Key switch open« is displayed on the touch screen.
3.Switch off main switch.
4.Disconnect the mains supply.
5.Remove the front panel of the PCM housing.
6.Loosen the safety interlock (2 turns).
7.Pull out the control unit using the handles.

Exchange of the Power Supply Modules
1.2 Exchange of the RF 24 V Power Supply (A204)
1.Remove the four fixing screws of the RF 24 V power supply on the bottom of the control unitand remove the power supply module.
2.Remove the cover and remove all terminals.
3.Connect all terminals to the new RF 24 V power supply module and mount the cover.
4.Insert the RF 24 V power supply module into the control unit and tighten the fixing screwson the bottom of the control unit.
1.3 Exchange of the -24 V Power Supply (A202)
1.Remove the fixing screws of the -24 V power supply on the bottom of the control unit andremove the power supply module.
2.Remove the cover and remove all terminals.
3.Connect all terminals to the new -24 V power supply module and mount the cover.
4.Insert the -24 V power supply module into the control unit and tighten the fixing screws onthe bottom of the control unit.

1.Remove the fixing screws of the +24 V power supply on the bottom of the control unit andremove the power supply module.

After exchanging the module(s) carry out the following steps:1.Push the control unit into the PCM.
2.Lock the control unit.
3.Mount the front panel of the PCM.
