through high - energy laser radiation.
• High radiation intensity causes a local heating and burning of the cornea and
thus a reduction or loss of sight!

Fire Hazard
• The high output power of a class 4 laser can inflame
various materials.
• Therefore, while performing maintenance and service
work at the open laser housing and/or open beam
delivery system, make sure that fire protection
measures were taken before starting the works!
• Never expose flammable materials to laser
radiation!
Always ensure appropriate shielding of the laser
beam! Errors during marking on flammable materials
(e.g. paper) might cause fires.

Electrical Safety
• Danger of explosion exists if battery is
incorrectly replaced.
• Replace only with the same or equivalent type.
• Dispose of used batteries according to the
battery manufacturer’s instructions.
• Power supply is on if laser is plugged in.

Over heating
• The laser system is cooled by air. The
internal cooling system is designed to supply
sufficient cooling.
• Make sure that the cooling air can be sucked
in and blown out freely and that there is
sufficient air exchange at the installation site
to ensure heat dissipation.

Fume hazard
• When printing on materials with laser radiation
by products are produced which are dangerous
to health!
• Through vaporization of materials fine dust and
vapors are produced which might include
hazardous byproducts depending on type of
material.
• It is highly recommend that a exhaust unit be
installed to prevents contamination and gradual
destruction of optical elements of the beam
delivery system by dust particles.

Lens hazard
The laser system contains optics constructed from Zinc Selenide. When broken
into small pieces or exposed to acidic conditions, they may give rise to harmful
products.
In normal use however, the laser optical components are not considered to be
hazardous.
In the event that a laser optical component is damaged in any way, the material
and any packaging must be treated as hazardous waste and disposed of
accordingly.

General Safety
• Min. bending radius of supply cable is 40 mm . The umbilical will be made for
stationary use. It will also sustain slow moving with low acceleration a few times a
day.
Before carrying out the maintenance works at the optics, please note
the following:
• Acetone is not included in delivery and has to be purchased via other companies.
Open the following Internet page www.vwr/index.htm to order acetone
in a quick and uncomplicated way.
• When purchasing acetone, make sure that you order acetone pro analysi
(p.a. = highest purity grade). Do not use IPA.
Never expose unknown materials to laser radiation!
Some materials (e.g. polyethylene, polypropylene, glass) are easily penetrated by the
laser beam, although they seem to be opaque for the human eye.

One noticeable characteristic is skirting of lines. You can almost see the dots in every

Marking intensity
The laser parameter marking intensity affects extremely the quality of the marking.
It alters the power of the laser marking system.
The marking intensity is adjustable from 0 to 100%.

Note the jagged line on “M”. The galvos have not stabilized yet and the laser is turned on.
This could be corrected by reducing the Jump Speed and or increasing Jump Delay.

Mark Delay to high
Curve marking are thick

Notice that characters like the “O”, “0” does not close, “M” have short legs.
The galvo is tracing the outline of the character but the laser is still of.
This could be adjusted with decreasing the Laser ON Delay.
On some sensitive materials a very low Laser On delay can puncture the substrate.
This sometimes could be mistaken as Laser off delay.
Take note where the start and stop of the strokes are.

On delay to high

Notice tails drilling on end of characters. The galvos jumped to the new vector and the
laser is still on. This can be adjusted by reducing the Laser Off delay.

Notice some segments of the characters are short and other like the “O” and “”0”
does not close. The galvos are still tracing the end of the characters but the laser is off
already. This can be corrected with increasing the Laser off delay.
