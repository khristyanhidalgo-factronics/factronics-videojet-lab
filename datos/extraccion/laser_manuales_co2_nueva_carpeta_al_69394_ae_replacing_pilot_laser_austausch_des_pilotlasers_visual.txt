1 Exchange of the Pilot Laser LC100/LC300/3120/3320 (SHC60)
The following is required for exchanging the pilot laser:
double offset screw driver 6-edged 3 mm
screw driver 6-edged 1.5 mm
1.1 Dismounting
DangerThe laser system must be disconnected from the power supply withoutfail before starting with the activity.
1.Turn the key-operated switch in the position »0«.Remove the key, in order to ensure that the laser system cannot be
switched-on.
2.Save the database in the software. For a detailed description see the manual of the software.
3.Turn-off the main switch.
4.Pull the mains plug.
NoteIf you have a system with straight beam path (0° version) you have toremove the marking head first (see instruction "Exchange of the marking
head").
5.Loosen the fastening screws of the marking head cover and keep opening the housing untilyou can disconnect the red light-emitting diode.
