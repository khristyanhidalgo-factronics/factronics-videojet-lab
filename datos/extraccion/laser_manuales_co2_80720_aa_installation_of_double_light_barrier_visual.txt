1 Installation of Double Light Barrier
Using the double light barrier it is possible to detect the product and the products speed withouta rotary encoder. The sensor automatically adjusts the line speed for each separate product.
This allows for a variable speed belt and for users greatest of ease.to move the printer from line to line with the
Important considerations for using a Double Light Barrier are:
Placement AccuracyFor the best results the product must come past the laser marking head and sensor consis-
tently. A constant belt speed is a key to get the best possible marking result . An oscillation of belt speed will have a negative impact on mark placement and marking quality. The dou-
ble light barrier should be placed as close as possible to the marking field borders, to use the latest real speed information for the marking.
Sensor CalibrationEach light sensor should be calibrated equally for necessary distance. That distance must
not exceed the depth of the conveyor. Through the turn potentiometer, it is possible to cali-brate the product distance and sensitivity for needed product surface.
Material ReflectivityThe speed sensor sensitivity must be set correctly. For more IR reflective products, a lower
sensitivity is recommended. For less IR reflecmended to minimize double trigger likelihood.tive products a higher sensitivity is recom-
Ambient Light RequirementsOverhead florescent tube or LED office lighting, no direct intense sunlight or incandescent
lighting onto front of sensors.

Product ShapeThe line speed sensor works by detecting a difference between nothing being there (low
amounts of infrared light reflected back into the sensor) and something being there (large amounts of infrared light reflected back). The sharpness of that transition is provided by the
shape and material of the item to be printed onto.
Strong sharp edge
This is the best shape for marking placement results.
Soft/rounded/draft edges
These products will require more adjustment of the sensiti-vity value and alterations to the nominal marking offset so
that the marking is placed in the correct position. Place-ment variability will also be larger but is in general still per-
fectly acceptable.
Odd shapes
A higher sensitivity is likely needed to ensure that doubletriggers do not take place. The item material should also be
more reflective.
