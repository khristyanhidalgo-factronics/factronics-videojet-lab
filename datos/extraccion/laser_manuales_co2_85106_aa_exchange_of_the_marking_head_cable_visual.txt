1.3 Mounting of the New Marking Head Cable
1.Carefully insert the cables into the marking head, starting with the biggest connector.
2.Insert the screw connection but do not tighten it yet.
3.Mount the temperature sensor using a 2.5 mm Allen key.
4.Connect the LED: red wire to +24V and violet wire to GND.
5.Carefully connect all connectors. Make sure to connect the output window sensor switch correctly.
Make sure the pin of the output window sensor switch is in place.
6.Tighten the screw connection.
7.Mount all covers to the marking head. Make sure the seals are in place.
8.Check the lens in the mounting plate. Clean it if necessary.
