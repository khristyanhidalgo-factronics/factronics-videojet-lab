TT O in W et Environments
How harsh an environment can a TT O printer operate in? TT O printers have a very
low IP rating due to the necessary open area where the print head comes into
contact with the substrate. Therefore, areas where wash down occurs will require the
printer to be installed with an IP65 housing, as will the operator interface, due to the
“D” type connections and communication ports. Dust or fluids on the substrate

could put the printhead at risk and cause excessive wear or thermal shock.
Therefore, the cleaner and drier the environment the better .
The enclosure is for wash down only the unit will not print with the IP65 enclosure in
place.

I n t e r m i t t e n t
Figure 2: Illustration of Registration Unit.
A registration unit allows the print position on the packaging film to be moved into the required area.
By adjusting the registration roller up or down, the length of the film between the supply reel and the
sealing jaws of the packaging machine is made longer or shorter . This changes the printing position on
the film accordingly .

The registration unit should, ideally , be positioned between the sealing jaws of the packaging machine
and the printer . This will ensure the print position can be changed when the registration roller position
is adjusted.
Registration units are used on the packaging film in intermittent applications. Occasionally , there are
continuous applications where the film is moving too quickly or too slowly for the 6530 to print. By
using the registration unit, it may be possible to adjust the film path to a faster or slower part of the
packaging cycle, allowing a suitable opportunity for printing with the 6530.
In order to achieve the best quality print from a continuous application, the substrate should wrap a
minimum of 90 degrees around the print roller .
Figure 3: Registration Roller Intermittent
Intermittent Considerations
When adopting the Modular System for intermittent packaging applications, the following information is
important and will af fect the specified solution outcome.
The width of the reel carriage, and therefore the maximum permissible substrate material, is a key
factor in specifying the required bracket width. The Modular System is designed with a flat pack
dimension of 1m wide (wider applications are rare but possible by specifying separately). The common
denomination to specify a bracket width is known as the A Dimension (all other cutting lengths on a
bracket width are arrived at by simple formulae derived from the A Dimension). The A Dimension is
the overall width of a fully assembled bracket, measured to the outside surface of the end
plates.
Where does the bracket go? Each Packaging machine will have a dif ferent web path configuration, but
all can be categorized into three groups. T emplates are available, which, when held up to the machine
can mimic the intended bracket position, verifying that appropriate space is available and its ef fect on
other reel gear on the machine. The majority of VFFS Machines allow mounting of a Modular System
on the top portion of the machine or above the supply reel gear , as shown in Figure 3 . Height
clearance above the machine (due to platform where the products are to be weighed) can sometimes
be limiting, depending on the type of product being packed. Where space is not available then the rear
of the machine is used but the further the coder is mounted from the sealing point of the bag means
that more bags can be wasted on changeover
Positioning of guide rollers will vary from application to application, but because they are “plug and
play” it is easy to adjust these rollers on installation. The criteria for the roller positioning are that they
must present the substrate in the correct plane to the print head and that also the web should not foul
on any stationary part of the bracket or machine frame, causing additional web drag.
The Modular System, when fitted to intermittent machines, must have adequate adjustment to print at
any point across the width of the bag or at any point along the length of the longest bag. The
adjustment across the width of the substrate is taken care of by manually moving the printer across
the bracket, and the adjustment up and down the bag length is achieved by implementing a
registration module. The registration module allows the stopping position of the film to change in
relation to the print head and is ef fected by adjustment of a moveable roller up and down a rack.
C o n t i n u o u s
Why do we need the registration roller in continuous mode?

Figure 4: Registration Roller Continuous
The majority of continuous applications do not require a registration unit as the substrate does not
stop moving, an electronic delay can be set within the 6530, when the delay expires, the coder
receives the signal to print. All continuous printers will require an encoder to match the substrate
speed to print speed.
Continuous Considerations
As with the intermittent considerations, it is important that the bracket is able to cope with the widest
substrate on the application. The A dimension should reflect this. On HFFS machines, which are
generally continuous, the reel gear is quite often cantilevered in design, as opposed to the traditional
VFFS machine which of fers bracket support at both ends. This does not cause a problem as the
Modular Bracket design is very strong. However , once the reel gear width increases beyond 600mm,
then it is common practice to brace the suspended end of the bracket. This is achieved either by
bracing to other rollers cantilevered on the parent machine or to the product transport table.
As with vertical machines it is essential that the coder is always mounted after the break dancing arm
(which is normally immediately after the supply reel and is intended to prevent reel overrun). On
continuous wrappers, however , it is also more common to find automatic splicing systems, for parent
machines which of fer a twin reel facility . It is also essential on these machines that the coder is
mounted after these assemblies.
For Registration adjustment along the pack length, this is normally af fected electronically either at the
coder , but more normally by the parent machine.
Another consideration when gathering information on continuous applications is whether the machine
runs plain or print registered film. Fitting of a print registration sensor needs to be a consideration
where high accuracy of print position is required and the wrapper has auto-bag length correction.
Divert Rollers change the path of the film, allowing it to be:
Diverted easily out of the existing film path
Move freely through the printer
Diverted back into the existing film path
