E3212: Dirty Optical Calibration Sensor Warning
There is potential for 6330/6530 53mm printers to have 1- 4 LEDs on the printhead PCB remain
constantly lit at all times. This can lead to an E3212: Dirty Optical Calibration Sensor warning.
The warning can be cleared and does not have a negative impact on printer performance. The
printer will still be operable even if the warning is not cleared.
Engineering has introduced a Printhead PCB with improved component protection and a more
resilient conformal coating that successfully underwent field testing at sites known to previously
have had recurrences of the issue.
The Printhead PCB part numbers will remain the same:
Part Number Description
Spare 53mm_ TT (iii) Printhead PCB - RH
407915
Spare 53mm_ TT (iii) Printhead PCB - LH
407916
l The LH version of the PCB was first introduced with printer serial number
1930811001TZH . The first spares batch cut in began with 19304 .
l The RH version of the PCB was first introduced with printer serial number
1935710002TZH . The first spares batch cut in began with 19357 .
l These changes pertain only to the 53mm printers as the 32mm and 107mm printers
launched with the more resilient Printhead PCB.
Symptoms
The primary symptom is that 1- 4 LEDs on the printhead PCB remain constantly lit at all times
and in some cases an E3212: Dirty Optical Calibration Sensor warning may also be displayed.

Corrective Action
l If a 6330/6530 printer exhibits the symptoms described above, the Printhead PCB can be
replaced with a printhead PCB from a batch after the listed cut in.
l If a 6330/6530 printer requires printhead PCB replacement for other reasons and the
printer has never exhibited the symptoms described above , you may use any remain -
ing printhead PCBs in your trunk stock from batches from before the cut in.
