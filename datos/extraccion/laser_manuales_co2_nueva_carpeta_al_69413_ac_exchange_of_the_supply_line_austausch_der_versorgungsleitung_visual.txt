1.2 Mounting
For the figures the following steps, please refer to section Dismounting.
1.2.1 Connecting the supply line with the laser head1.Insert the individual cables from the outside through the opening of the laser head carefully.
2.Tighten the screwed cable gland.
3.First of all put the lock washer, the ring terminal and then the second lock washer onto thescrew thread. Afterwards tighten the screw.
Check the correct order.
4.Connect the connectors of the laser signal, power supply and fan.
5.Connect all the cables of the FM board.
6.Connect the connectors for the pilot laser (connectors X117 and X118).
7.Mount the housing cover of the laser head.
1.2.2 Connecting the supply line with the supply unit1.Insert the individual cables from the outside through the opening of the supply unit carefully.
2.Fasten the screwed cable gland from the inside with the nut.
3.Insert the ring terminal of the PE cable on the grub screw and fasten the nut.
4.Connect the two upper push-on connectors with the relay.
5.Connect all cables of the 100 W switching power supply.
6.Connect all cables of the 320 W switching power supply.
7.Connect all the connectors to the ACC board.
8.Fasten the housing cover and side cover of the supply unit.
