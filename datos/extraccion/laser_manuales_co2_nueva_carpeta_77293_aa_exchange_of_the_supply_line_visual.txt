1.2 Mounting
For the figures of the following steps, please refer to section Dismounting.
1.2.1 Connecting the supply line to the laser head1.Connect the supply line connector to the laser head.
1.2.2 Connecting the supply line to the supply unit1.Carefully insert the individual cables from the outside through the opening of the supply unit.
2.Fasten the screwed cable gland from the inside with the nut.
3.Insert the CP-Light cables at X28. Wire 6 at pin 6 and wire 5 at pin 12.
4.Insert the cables for 30 V (red) and GND (black) in terminal block X200.
5.Connect the connectors of LIC and HIC.
6.Make sure that all cables are laid properly and tidily using cable ties.
7.Close the cover of the supply unit.
NoteIf the new supply line has a different length than the previous one it might benecessary to adjust the voltage. See instructions next page.
