1.2 Mounting
1.Insert the adapter module into the PCM housing and tighten the fixing screws.
2.Mount the female connector.
3.Connect the connector of the DI compressor chiller to the female connector (interface X 301).
4.Connect all cooling water hoses to the adapter module (see figure on previous page).Observe the color code of the hoses (initial water: blue, return water: red)!
5.Mount the front panel of the PCM housing.
