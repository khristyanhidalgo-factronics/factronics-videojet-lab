Adjustment of the Diverging Lens
20.Center the laser beam on the cross wire using the xy adjusting screws.
21.Select »Shutter closed« in the Smart Graph software.
22.Loosen the adapter ring.
23.Rotate the diverging lens until the counter screws face upwards.
24.Tighten both counter screws.
25.Rotate the diverging lens back to its original position, reference edge facing upwards (seefigure on previous page).
26.Tighten the adapter ring.
27.Select »Shutter open« in the Smart Graph software.
28.Make sure that the laser beam is still centered on the indicator disk.
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be dead!
29.Turn the key switch to position »0«.Remove the key to ensure that the laser system cannot be switched on.
30.Wait until the message »Key switch open« is displayed on the touch screen.
31.Switch off main switch.
32.Disconnect the mains supply.
33.Close the housing cover of the laser head.
34.Remove the adjusting device and mount the marking head (see section "Mounting and Dis-mounting of the Marking Head" in this chapter).
