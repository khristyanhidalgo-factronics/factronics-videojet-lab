Exchange of the Shutter and Aperture Unit DN-A

1 Exchange of the Shutter and Aperture Unit
The shutter has to be exchangedif the shutter is no longer closing (indicated by the error message »E29009: Beam shutter
error«).The aperture has to be exchanged
if the aperture wheel does not rotate correctly.if the adjusting screws are not working.
The following is required for exchanging the shutter and aperture unithex key 2.5 mm
of the accessory kit:hex key 3 mm and 4 mm (00478 and 66918)
1.1 Dismounting the Shutter Unit
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be dead!1.Turn the key switch to position »0«.
Remove the key to ensure that the laser system cannot be switched on.
2.Wait until the message »Key switch open« is displayed on the touch screen.
3.Switch off main switch.
4.Disconnect the mains supply.
1.Open the housing cover of the laser head.
2.Loosen the screws of the cable duct cover and remove the cover.
3.Disconnect the connector X 408 of the shutter unit from the LHC.
connection shutter unit

4.Remove the fixing screws of the shutter unit cover and remove the shutter unit.
fixing screws ofshutter unit
1.2 Dismounting the Aperture Unit
5.Remove the silicone hose between pump chamber and aperture.Remove the dust protection tubes between aperture and Q-switch.
6.Remove the fixing screws of the aperture unit and remove the unit.
fixing screws ofaperture unit

Exchange of the Shutter and Aperture Unit DN-A
1.3 Mounting of Aperture Unit
1.Insert the aperture unit and tighten the fixing screws.
2.Place the beam protection tube between aperture and Q-switch.Place the silicone hose between pump chamber and aperture.
1.4 Mounting of Shutter Unit
3.Insert the shutter unit and tighten the fixing screws.
4.Plug connector X 408 of the shutter unit to the LHC and lock the connection.
5.Close the cover of the cable duct and tighten all screws.
ImportantAfter exchanging the shutter and aperture unit the mode aperture mustbe adjusted (see section "Adjustment of the Mode Aperture").
