Laser Technical Terms
Pump Chamber : Consists of laser lamp, resp. laser diode array and Nd: YAG-rod.
Generates together with the outcoupling and end mirro r the radiaton.
Service Training DN50A_LN100A
Resonator : All optical components between outcoupler and end mir ror.
Q-Switch : RF-controlled optical switch. Turns off the laser beam for the jumps and
turns on before the marking vectors. Generates the pulses in pulsed mode operation.
Mode Aperture : Varies the laser beam diameter and with that, the b eam quality.
Beam-Shutter : Electro-mechanical safety feature. Interrupts the laser beam inside
the resonator, if the interlock circuit is opened or the laser is stopped.
Expansion module : Expands the laser beam. This leads to a smaller focus poi nt.
Scanner head : Consists of two scanner mirrors. These deflect the laser b eam
according the template, to x-and y-positions.
Objective : Focuses the expanded laser beam onto the product surface (focus point).

Booting the System
After Power on, the System starts the initializatiothe following steps: n and goes through
The Power PC on the ACC-2 starts the bootloader prog ram from the boot flash and initializes the
ACC-2 Hardware. It loads a default image into the FP GA that makes it possible to access the MMC
Service Training DN50A_LN100A
in the next step.
The Power PC loads the LINUX image from the MMC and starts it.
LINUX is booted and initializes some parts of the ACC-2 and loads drivers for the hardware.
The start script of LINUX starts the Smart OS.
The Smart OS reads the system database from the EEPROM and the application database from the
MMC and stores them in the volitile memory of the SDRA M.
The Smart OS transfers an updated image to the FPGA and the program for the DSP to the ACC-2s
Digital Signal Processor (DSP).
Meanwhile the MDBs on the LHC and LPC have started th eir own bootloader programs and are
waiting to receive their application over the CAN-Bus.
The Smart OS checks the Versions of LHC and LPC and sends the appropriate programs to them.
The ACC-2 changes the system state to KEYSWITCH OPEN.
