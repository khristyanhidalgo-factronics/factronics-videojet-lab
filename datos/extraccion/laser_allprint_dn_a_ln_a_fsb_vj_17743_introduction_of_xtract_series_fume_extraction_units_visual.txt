As of September 2021, Xtract-Series fume extraction units are introduced as replacements for the
formerly available AD-Series fume extraction units.
See Obsolescence Notice - BOF A AD Fume Extraction Units for details on the AD-Series
obsolescence.
Please be aware of the following changes with the introduction of the Xtract extraction units, including
changes to mains cables, I/O cables, wiring, and extraction hose kits.
Information on spare parts and accessories is found in the VTI Support & T echnology SharePoint
Online site at Service Resources\Service Content\Laser\Xtract Fume Extractors .
