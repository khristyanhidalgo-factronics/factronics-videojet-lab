Applies to models: 7340, 7440.
As part of troubleshooting bad print quality or potential broken components, it may be necessary to check the power out of
the marking unit. The easiest place to take the power measurement is directly at the end of the marking head. If low or no
power is observed at the output of the marking unit, it may then become necessary to take of f the marking unit and
measure power directly out of the collimator to determine if low power is due to laser source or due to components in
marking unit blocking the laser beam.

This procedure requires special equipment. Only use the AL-82208 (Power Meter Y AG/CO2, Assembly) or AL-SP67438
Power meter (Laser Point Power Meter; discontinued). Do not use the Synrad Power W izard Power Meter . The Power
Wizard is not meant for the wavelength emitted by fiber lasers and can give inaccurate readings.
