GETTING STARTED
Initial set-up
1.
Open all cartons & locate Hoselclamps & Wheels
2.
Remove ALL filters & packing materials from cartons
3.
Place unit on its side & install wheels in bottom plate
4.
Remove plastic wrap from gas filter & place in base of unit — note direction of airflow arrow
5.
Place HEPA filter on top of gas cell — note airflow arrow
6.
Place Hold-Down Bracket on top of the HEPA filter
7.
Place pleated panel filter inside hold-down bracket
8.
Place polyester —pre-filter on top of panel filter
9.
Close and latch lid
Lo cating the air cleaner:
Placing the unit as close as possible to the pollution source reduces the amount of hose or duct needed,
reduces back-pressure and power consumption and improves overall performance.
Wheels are standard equipment on the FAI, but rubber isolators are available if the unit is to be placed
permanently in position. If you prefer isolators, call 800-432-7550 to arrange a no-charqe exchanqe.
Inlet h ose attachment:
Place the flex hose over the metal air inlet tube on top of the unit and tighten securely with
the stainless Steel clamp.
Place the other end of the hose, or suction hood, as close as possible to the pollution source.
Electric power
FAI 's are manufactured for use on 115/1/60 (5.7 amps) or 230/1/60 (3.8 amps) power.
Confirm that correct power supply is available.

GENERAL ALTER CARE
Fumex FAI air purifiers feature a multi-stage filtration system designed to remove a Wide range of
pollutants from the process plume in an extremely efficient, cost-effective manner. The sytem utilizes a
pressure differential switch to indicate a blocked/ clogged filter. Once the warning light illuminates, filters
should be checked and clogged filters replaced.
STANDARD FILTERS:
Part # 103-6 — Polyester pre-filter
The disposable polyester pre-filter is the first stage of the filtration process. Its main function is to
remove dust particles 100B and larger in advance of the more expensive pleated panel filter.
Replacement:
The service life of the polyester pre-filter is determined by the type and volume of large particles
collected.
Average service life is 2 —4 weeks
Part # FA102 - Pleated Panel Filter
Pleated panel filters are merv 11 efficiency for use in removing particles as small as IOB from the
pollution stream. Particles of this size are respirable and can cause respiratory problems in
concentrations exceeding OSHA mandated levels.
Replacement:
A pleated panels' service life is determined by the volume of particles remaining in the air stream after
passing through the polyester pre-filter. When properly protected by the pre-filter, and depending on
loading, pleated panel filters service life is 4-6 weeks.
Part # FAIOO - HEPA Filter
The HEPA filter is 99.97% efficient in removing particles as small as 0.3B from an airstream.
Particles of this size are respirable and can cause significant respiratory problems.
Replacement:
A HEPA's service life is determined by the volume of sub-micron particles remaining in the airstream
after passing through the pre-filters. When properly protected, and depending on loading, HEPA filters
often provide months of service. Average service life is 2-4 months.
Part # FAIOID — Adsorbent Cell
Most process plumes contain several gases, some odorous — some not, some toxic at elevated
concentrations — some completely harmless regardless of concentration. To ensure employee safety the
type and concentration of pollutants must be determined. Please contact Fumex Engineering for
selection assistance.
The FAI utilizes a 5 lb. blended activated carbon and activated alumina impregnated with KMn04 cell to
capture gases/odors. This combination of materials provides protection across a broad range of gases
commonly found in industrial manufacturing processes.
Replacement:
The FAI is fitted with a disposable saturation indicator used to monitor the efficiency of the adsorbent
cell. Pollutants in the exhaust stream Will cause the pellets to change color from purple to brown.
Replace the adsorbent cell when color change is complete. A new indicator Will be supplied with the
replacement adsorbent cell or can be ordered from Fumex (Part # FA202). The indicator is attached with
Velcro to the front panel and is easily replaced.

2) Remove loose accumulations of dust/debris from the air filter chamber periodically with a shop vac.
