The Advantage 180LC has all the features of the Advantage 180 with theaddition of filtered cooling air. The unit is fitted with a separate blower located
at the front of the system. Air is drawn in through a series of louvres on thefront face of the system and is blown through a cylindrical filter, through a
flexible hose and into the laser. When the filter requires replacing an indicationlight will illuminate.

The Advantage 180 has been designed with cost of ownership and long termreliability in mind. The side channel blower within the extractor gives double
the airflow and pressure of its proven predecessor, the L20M. With this extraperformance coupled with BOFAÕs unique 2 stage pre filter and unit design we
are able to offer a 3 year warranty on all units as well as an unrivalled filter life.Filter replacement is made easy by simply opening a door to expose both filter
compartments. This ensures a quick and clean filter change, minimising costlydown time.
