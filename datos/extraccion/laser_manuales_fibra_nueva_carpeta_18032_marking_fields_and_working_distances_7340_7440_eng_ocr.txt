Marking Fields and Working Distances (7340, 7440)
The marking field, the largest possible area that is possible to be marked by the laser, is determined
by the marking head unit installed on the laser system. Each marking head unit has different lenses
glued inside that create both the spot size and marking field size
Figure 1: Orientation of A and B dimensions of marking fields.
Working Distance is the recommended distance from the marking head to the product. T his is the
point where the beam has the most density (concentration of energy). The best distance for the
marking unit to the product may occasionally vary in practice based on the material being marked and
the desired effect. The best working distance can vary across the marking field because the lenses
used in the 7340/7440 are not F-theta lenses.

Turned Head Orientation
Note that the correct printing orientation of the CFT (turned) marking head Will place the beam window
at a slight angle. When correctly oriented, the straight line on the end of the head Will be vertically
aligned as shown in Eigure 3. Therefore, the window should not be parallel to the product as the
mirrors produce a slightly angled output.
