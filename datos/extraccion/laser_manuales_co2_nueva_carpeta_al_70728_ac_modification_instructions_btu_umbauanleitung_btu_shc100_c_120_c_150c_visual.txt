1.1.3 Mounting the Marking Head and BTU Marking Head Cable on the Marking Head1.Fasten the connection piece in the rectangular beam path (90°version) or in the straight
beam path (0°version) on the marking head.
2.Carefully pull the individual leads of the BTU marking head cable from outside through theopening of the marking head.
3.Tighten the screwed cable gland.
4.Fasten the marking head onto the BTU using the body clamp.
5.Insert the Galvo block into the marking head. If there is a pilot laser connect the pilot laser to the pilot laser connector (connectors X122
and X123).
6.Mount the Galvo block by tightening the fixing screws.
7.Connect both scanner connectors.The X scanner cable is marked with a blue shrink-on hose.
8.Connect the connectors of the LED.Make sure that the red cable of the LED is connected to the connector of the 24 V supply.
9.Close the housing cover and tighten the fixing screws.
10.Fasten the focusing optics on the marking head.
