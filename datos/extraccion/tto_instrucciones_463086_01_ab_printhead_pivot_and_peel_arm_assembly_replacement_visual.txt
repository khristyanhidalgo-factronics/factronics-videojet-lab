assembly and its holders. Insert the pivot shaft, with its head facing upwards, into the printhead pivot assembly. Secure the pivot shaft with the two screws.
7Install the other end of the printhead belt into the printhead idler pulley (item 5,
Figure 11 on page 9 ). Secure the printhead idler pulley, along with printhead belt,
on the idler shaft using the screw.
8Align the printhead on its guides and slide it towards the printhead pivot assembly.
