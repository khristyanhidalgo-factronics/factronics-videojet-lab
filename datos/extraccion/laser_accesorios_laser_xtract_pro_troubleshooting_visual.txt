Xtract Pro – trouble shooting
1) Door
 Inspect the door seal/gasket for damage, the gasket should be intact and clean.
 In the upper left-hand corner, inspect the protruding hose is intact and not blocked.
2) Filter (AL-72981) – Xtract Pro Primary/Pre Filter [RoHS]
 Remove the filter from the extractor unit.
 Inspect and verify the large black seal is intact and clean.
 Inspect and verify the protruding flange where the filter mates, are free of burrs and is
clean.
3) Filter (AL-72982) - Xtract Pro Combined Filter [RoHS]
 Release the handle which is in the unlocked (nine o’clock) position, this releases the
clamping force from the seal.
 Remove the Combined filter and inspect the rubber gasket located inside on the top is
intact and clean.
 Install the combined filter, lock the filter in place by lifting the handing (three o’clock)
position in the locked position, this elevates the filter and creates the seal with the unit.
 Note: when the clamping pressure is unlocked (released), with the filter in place, when
starting the unit, this will produce an error code 128 (no combined filter).
 See picture below:

4) Remove the front panel
 Open the front door.
 Remove the front panel (remove the three screws, using a 2.5 mm allen wrench).
 Place the “Stop/Start Function” switch in the stop position.
 Off position – the unit operates as a standalone unit.
 On position – the unit operates remotely with the laser unit.
 Note: during trouble shooting we can use the On and Off positions to start and
stop the unit.
 Note: we can use the Standby button located to the left of the display located on the
membrane to turn on and off the unit.
 Red illumination – the extractor is switched off.
 Green illumination – the extractor is switched on.
 Inspect the input hoses, five in total. Three in the front and two located on the side of the
plenum. See picture below:

5) Consider replacing the “Xtract Pro/PVC/Max PCB” board (AL-81052).
