AL-79735Service Part Installation Instruction
Exchange of the Power Supply Modules
1.2.3 Mounting a new Laser Power Supply ModuleFor the figures concerning the following steps, please refer to section Exchange of Laser
Power Supply Module (350 W) on page 6.
1.Insert the laser power module.
2.Tighten the four fixing screws.
3.Connect the cables to the secondary side of the module.Make sure that the feedback lines are mounted in V-shape.
4.On the primary side mount the three cables (green/yellow = PE, blue = AC/N, black = AC/L).
5.Mount the touch protection.
6. If the system is equipped with CPD3 and FASS (instead of SPM):Insert the FASS on its mounting plate and tighten the four fixing screws.
7.Insert the mounting base back into the housing and push it to the back.
8.Tighten the four fixing screws of the mounting base.
9.At the back of the supply unit tighten the screws of the power connector and the screw thatattaches the mounting base to the back of the supply unit.
10.Connect the supply connectors to the CP-Light board.
11.Mount the grounding cables.
NoteFirst mount the grounding cable of the SPM/CPD3 board directly to thehousing of the supply unit.
Then mount the grounding cable coming from the grounding bolt of themounting base on top of the SPM/CPD3 grounding cable.
12.Insert the two cables of the laser power supply module into the terminal block.
13.Connect all connectors to the Chameleon board.
14.Mount the LIC, HIC and MIC (if available) onto the Chameleon board.
15.Close the housing cover.

grounding of mounting base

6.If the system is equipped with CPD3 and FASS (instead of SPM):Insert the FASS on its mounting plate and tighten the four fixing screws.
7.Place the mounting base in the supply unit so that you can connect the grounding cable fromthe lower mounting base.
8.Insert the mounting base back into the housing and push it to the back.
NoteMake sure that the flat ribbon cable of the LIC is not pinched or trapped.
9.Tighten the four fixing screws of the mounting base.
10.At the back of the supply unit tighten the screws of the power connector and the screw thatattaches the mounting base to the upper back panel of the supply unit.
11.Connect the supply connector to the CP-Light board.
