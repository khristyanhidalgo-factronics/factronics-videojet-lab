Fume Extractor Maintenance
Based on Fume Extractor (FE) market research , we have identified th at some customers are not aware of regular
FE filter maintenance, are confused with filter status indicator lights, have issues with filter part numbers, and need
proper ordering information. To help with FE maintenance, we have developed the following materials:
• Fume Extractor Preventive Maintenance guide has been developed
• The Filter Status Indicator table and FE Filter log located in the manual have been redesigned to be easy
to understand and are now available as labels to be placed on the Fume Extractor
• New Fume Extractor website for North America

Email/CSE Campaign
There will be 3 phases introduced over the next 6 months.
1. The first phase will be an email to introduce the new FE website to current customers along with links to
FE PM Guide and FE Labels.
2. The second phase will involve our inside sales team who will calls current customers to confirming they
are aware of the new FE resources available to them. Coinciding with this our service techs in the field will
carry the new FE labels with them while visiting FE customers.
3. The last phase will involve strategic emailing and phone calls to remind our customers about regular FE
filter replacement.
