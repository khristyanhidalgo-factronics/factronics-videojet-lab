T o u c h U . S . - T e c h n i c a l S p e c s
Stainless steel touch panel designed for use with 3340/3140/3640 laser systems.
The IP rating is IP65 and has 2 USB ports.
The touch panel comes standard with TCS software, the same software which runs on 3020
will also run with the 3340/3140/3640. Smartgraph can run from the touch panel,
Smartgraph needs to be ordered separately , using part number AL-72846, Smartgraph USB
stick.
The T ouch US will not run two (2) lasers or more from one panel. The T ouch US is a
 one touch panel to one laser .
When ordering the touch panel, order part number AL-76397 (10 and 30 watt models) or AL-
78253 (60 watt model), 8-Pole Connector for User interface. This part number provides
power to the T ouch U.S as the T ouch U.S does not come with a power cord.
Applicable with 3340 ,3140, 3640 lasers. The T ouch U.S is capable of mounting to the
standard H-Base stand. Additional mounting option for the T ouch U.S is available, this
mounting bracket is able to mount to the H-Base stand or sit independently on a table as
required, part number AL-72835..
T ouch U.S T ouch Panel comes with a 5 m (16.404 feet) cable for 3140 and 3340 models –
AL-77335, in addition order part number AL-76397 8-Pole Connector .
T ouch U.S T ouch Panel with 5 m (16.404 feet) cable for 3640 – AL-78219, in addition order
AL-78253 8-Pole connector .
