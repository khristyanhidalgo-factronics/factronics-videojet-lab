2 Programming of the MMC
1.Install the card reader according to the card readers manual. After that you will see the cardreader as a new disc drive.
2.Insert the MMC into the card reader and open the Windows Explorer.
3.In the Windows Explorer open the drive that refers to the card reader.
4.Save the file database.ydb to your hard disk.
5.Close the drive in the Windows Explorer.
6.Select the drive by clicking with the right mouse key and format the MMC.
7.Open the zip-file from the appropriate QMark directory and extract all the files to the MMC.
8.Copy the saved file database.ydb to the directory "Smarties" and overwrite the existing file.
