1 Modification with BTU for 10 W Laser System (SHC60(C))
The following is required for modification of the BTU:
offset hex key 3 mm and 2.5 mm
hex key 1.5 mm
spanner 22 mm
1.1 Installation of BTU
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be dead!
1.Turn the key-operated switch in the position »0«.Remove the key, in order to ensure that the laser system cannot be
switched-on.
2.Save the database in the software. For a detailed description see the software.
3.Turn-off the main switch.
4.Pull the mains plug.
1.1.1 Preparation1.Remove the FM board (see instruction "Exchange of the FM/CPLight board").
2.Remove the marking head cable (see instruction "Exchange of the Marking Head Cable").
3.Remove the marking head (see instruction "Exchange of the Marking Head").
WichtigAt straight beam path the built-in adapter front plate must be exchanged withthe adapter front plate of the modification kit.
4.Carefully pull the individual leads of the BTU marking head cable from the modification kitfrom outside through the opening of the laser head.
5.Tighten the screwed cable gland.
