Wavelength Vs. the Eye
Laser radiation in the invisible, short wave UV - A range (wavelength
315 nm - 380 nm) of low intensity can cause inflammation of the
cornea ( photokeratitis ) or of the conjunctiva ( photoconjunctivitis )
as well as cataract (clouding of the eye lens). In case of higher
radiation intensity it can cause reversible corneal opacity,
irreversible corneal opacity and cataracts.

Photokeratitis
http://informationng.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/12/photokeratitis.jpg
Photokeratitis or ultraviolet keratitis is a painful eye condition caused by exposure of
insufficiently protected eyes to the UV rays from either natural or artificial sources.
Photokeratitis is akin to a sunburn of the cornea and conjuntiva , and is not usually
noticed until several hours after exposure. Symptoms include increased tears and a
feeling of pain, likened to having sand in the eyes.

Lens hazard
The laser system contains optics constructed from Zinc Selenide. When broken into small
pieces or exposed to acidic conditions, they may give rise to harmful products.
In normal use however, the laser optical components are not considered to be hazardous.
In the event that a laser optical component is damaged in any way, the material and any
packaging must be treated as hazardous waste and disposed of accordingly.

UV Laser PPE Controls
1. Engineering controls (enclosure and Barriers)
• Enclose UV laser beam paths to extent practical
• Design barriers or enclosures for beam dumps to minimize potential diffuse
exposures.
2. Administrative Procedures
• Attenuate laser beam and plan work to minimize exposure time
• Keep exposed skin as far as practical from open beams
3. Skin PPE
• Wear long sleeved shirts
• Use gloves when working with hands accessible laser beams (primary or stray)
• Use gloves when diffuse reflection NHZ> 20cm, if working within that distance
• Use face shield when diffuse reflection NHZ> 1m if working within that distance
4. Medical exams
• Periodic skin exams recommended for laser personnel who may have exposures >
MPE
5. Site specific training
• OJT and SOP must describe potential for skin injury and controls to use

Some Notes on Source & Warm Up Time
• Expected lifetime of the laser beam source: 15,000 - 25,000 hours. But warranty is for 2
years.
• Marking Intensity less than approx. 75%, leads to an unstable marking.
• Laser On Delay: 7 minutes. (After having closed the key - switch.)
So don‘t turn off key very often like we would have recommended on other lasers.
• Each time you make an adjustment to Intensity it will take up to 1 minute for the laser
source to adjust. That is why you will rarely see indexes with different Intensity
settings used in UV applications.
• After the first marking wait for 2 hours until the beam source runs stable.
Only neccessary for very high quality requirements (2D barcodes, etc.) and after the
system was turned off for several hours.
Neccessary to warm up the SHG / THG crystals in source to approx. 40 ° C in order
to achieve optimal conversion and a good output power stability.
• Regular applications don‘t need 2 hour warm - up time, but remember that each time
you open key switch it will take about 7 minutes to recover and that anytime you
adjust Intensity it will require a delay.

Special Considerations for 7810 Site Survey
• Does Sample Report give working distance? UV may use working distance that is different from the
manual. Often need to make slight adjustment in distance to get desired effect on specific material.
Don’t just go by manual!!
• When mounted to mark on side of product you don’t have much adjustment in/out for focusing. Make
sure measurements are correct for distance from product.
• Is there enough space by the conveyor? (Remember this laser is very large)
• Will we be using a stand or will we be fabricating a bracket (Regular stand won’t work with UV — too large
and heavy). There is SAR H - based stand available.
• Where will the controller be located?
• Will you need an environmental cabinet for the controller (IP21 supply unit/IP20 marking head)?
• Will the standard extractor work (even when you don’t see smoke you can still be generating gas)?
• Ensure you only consider mounting laser head horizontally and also not upside down (even if horizontal).
• Do you need a BTU? Remember only SS10 head has one available and it is an SAR.
• What kind of safety switch will be used? If FASS board, you will need additional reset switch if wiring as
Interlock with FASS. (7810 has CPD standard and FASS available as upgrade).
