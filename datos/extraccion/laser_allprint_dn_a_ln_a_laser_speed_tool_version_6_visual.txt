How to use this Excel table …
1 Sheet marking fields: How to determine the usable marking field length
In order to determine the usable marking field length of a respective application, please enter the max. heigth of the whole mark into the
appropriate field in the sheet "marking fields". Please only enter this value in the one line, that responds to the appropriate laser with the
right lense. The possible marking field length is then calculated automatically in the red highlighted field.
In some areas, the heigth of the mark has no influence on the possible marking field length.
*The Focus series has fixed, square marking fields. These are shown above on the same sheet.
2 Sheets "Speed -> Time" resp. "Time -> Speed"
Please enter the marking field length and all other required values/ data in these two sheets. (For the marking field length, take the value
you got from the sheet "marking fields".)
The "product pitch" is not the distance/ pitch btw. two products! In this field, you have to enter the pitch between. the front border of two sequential
marks (see drawing).
The respective result automatically appears in the red field.
… and what to consider:
All values/ data in this table have to be determined exactly as they have a major influence on the feasabilty of applications.
This tool is only applicable for marking alphanumerical signs, not for logos.
