It has been noticed that when installing/removing ribbon on new TT O printers and/or new cassettes,
it seems to require a bit more force to do so. This is due to the quality of leaf springs not being the
same quality as in years past.
In regards to the 9550, there have been cases where the ribbon cores slip on the mandrels.

I n t e r n a l C o m m e n t s
TT O - This escalation is due to the quality of leaf springs making it so that more force is needed to
install and/or remove ribbon - but ultimately it can be removed by hand. It is not meant for typical
'tight core' issues where the waste ribbon winds so tight that sometimes extreme measures are
required to remove it.
LP A - Ribbon cores slip on mandrels which can then cause poor print quality and/or ribbon related
faults. This too is due to the quality of the leaf springs.

M i t i g at i o n / W o r kar o u n d ( T T O P r i n t er s)
If someone truly finds it too dif ficult to install/remove ribbon:
After a few ribbon core insertions/removals, the leaf springs should naturally 'flatten' a bit
and make it a bit easier to install/remove.
The leaf springs could be 'flattened' a very tiny bit by using pliers to carefully squeeze the
leaf spring with little force. Flattening them too much can then have the negative
effect that they can no longer reliably grip the core and thus the core will slip and
potentially cause poor print and/or ribbon related faults.
