1 Modification of the Cooling Unit
Due to EMC problems caused by the switching of valve Y1 a solid state relay must be mountedin the cooling unit. All parts required for the modification are included in the modification kit (part
no. 67655). The wiring diagrams are included at the end of this document.
1.1 Preparation
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be dead!1.Turn the key switch to position »0«.
Remove the key to ensure that the laser system cannot be switched on.
2.Wait until the message »Key switch open« is displayed on the touch screen.
3.Switch off main switch.
4.Disconnect the mains supply.
1.Remove the strain relief plate of the cooling hoses at the back of the PCM.
2.Remove the front panel of the PCM housing.
3.Remove the fixing screws in the bottom plate and of the supply module and pull out the coolingunit until the terminal box is accessible from the side.

1.3 Mounting
1.Insert the cooling unit into the PCM housing so that the terminal box remains accessible.
2.Push the cover of the terminal box through the opening in the bottom plate of the cooling unit.
3.Mount the female connector.
4.Connect connector X 300 to the female connector.
5.Connect the 14-pole female connector to the CTP.
6.Close the cover of the terminal box and tighten the screws.
7.Push the cooling unit into the PCM housing up to the stop.Make sure that the black cooling hoses are not bent or squeezed!
8.Fix the cooling unit using the fixing screws.
9.Lock the supply unit and tighten the fixing screw.
10.Mount the front panel of the PCM housing.
11.Mount the strain relief plate of the cooling hoses at the back of the PCM.
