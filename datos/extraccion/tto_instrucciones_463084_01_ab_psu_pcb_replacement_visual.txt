How to Install the PSU PCB
1Reconnect the 48V power supply cable and 15 pin I/O ribbon cable ( Figure 6 on
page 4 ) to the bottom of the PCB.
2Carefully place the new PCB and position ththe PSU. Secure the connectors using four screwlocks (e connectors through the front side of

3Place the earth wire on the PSU cover and secure it using the nut ( Figure 4 on
page 3 ).
4Position the PSU cover on the PSU and secure it using the four screws ( Figure 3
on page 3 ).
5Ensure that the connector PCB (item 1, Figure 3 on page 3 ) connected firmly to the
PSU PCB.
6Place the PSU rear cover on the unit and secure it using the six screws (item 8,
Figure 2 on page 2 ).
7Reconnect all the connectors to the PSU ( Figure 2 on page 2 ).
8Carefully replace the LCD display on the PSU ( Figure 1 on page 2 ).
9Reconnect the mains lead to the PSU.
10Turn on the printer power supply at the PSU power switch.
The installation of the PSU PCB is complete.
