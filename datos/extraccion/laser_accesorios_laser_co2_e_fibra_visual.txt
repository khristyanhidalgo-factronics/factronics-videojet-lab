NEW LASER ACCESSORY:
Alert Light Kit for Videojet Laser Marking Systems
We are pleased to announce the immediate availability of a new Alert Light Kit accessory that is compatible with the
Videojet 3120, 3320, 3430 (CO2) and 7210/7310 (Fiber) laser marking systems.
Alert Lights – or “beacons” – are often used in manufacturing to provide visual indication of product line status. With
the addition o f the alert light, supervisors or managers can see “at a glance” laser marking systems that are on - line
and marking, off - line or in a fault condition.
The Alert Light Kit features:
• Two large, bright (Red/Green) indicators, with steady on/off (no n - flashing) operation
• LED lamps that have lower power consumption and longer life than incandescent bulbs
• Direct connection to the laser marking system outputs, to simplify wiring and installation
The Kit contains a complete set of har dware for 3 mounting styles:
• Through - hole mounting (useful for mounting to the top of the cabinet made of sheet metal)
• Surface mounting using a flange base (useful for thicker surfaces, like tables)
• Right - angle (L) bracket mou nting (useful for walls, sides of cabinets, etc.)
Both short (43 mm) and long (240 mm) poles are included to position the light close to or further away from the
mounting surface.
