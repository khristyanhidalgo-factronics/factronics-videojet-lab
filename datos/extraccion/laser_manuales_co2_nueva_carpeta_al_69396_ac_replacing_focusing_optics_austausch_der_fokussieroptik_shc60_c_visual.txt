AL-69396Service Part Installation Instruction
Exchange of the Focusing Optics
1 Exchange of the Focusing Optics
The following is required for exchanging the focusing optics:
Double offset screw driver 6-edged 2.5 mm
1.1 Dismounting
DangerThe laser system must be disconnected from the power supply withoutfail before starting with the activity.
1.Turn the key-operated switch in the position »0«.Remove the key, in order to ensure that the laser system cannot be
switched-on.
2.Save the database in the software. For a detailed description see the manual of the software.
3.Turn-off the main switch.
4.Pull the mains plug.
ImportantAlways wear gloves while carrying out these activities!
The focusing optics is in a holder with a quarter-turn fastener. The quarter-turn fastener isunlocked after a quarter turn.
1.Carefully rotate the focusing optics anti-clockwise by a quarter turn.Do not touch the surface of the focusing optics with the gloves!
2.Remove the focusing optics from the marking head and place it on a clean surface.
3.Loosen the both fastening screws and remove the thrust collar.
4.Take out the focusing optics.

1.2 Mounting
1.Take the new focusing optics between thumb and index finger and put it in the holder.
2.Fasten the thrust collar.
3.Insert the focusing optics in the marking head.
4.Carefully rotate the focusing optics clockwise by a quarter turn.Do not use any tools!
