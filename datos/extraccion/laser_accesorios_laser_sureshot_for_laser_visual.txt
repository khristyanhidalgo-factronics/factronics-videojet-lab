SURE SHOT FOR LASER
Confidential - Company Proprietary

1. DESCRIPTION
Sure Shot is a device normally used with two CIJ printers in order to put them printing at the same
production line, once at a time alternately, while both machines are running (one prints on the first
product, the other prints on the second product and so on)
If one of the printers enters in fault , the Sure Shot sends all the print signals to the other one . To do
this , Sure Shot uses a Print Ready signal from both machines .
Confidential - Company Proprietary

2. Using Sure Shot with VJ laser printers
In some laser applications , where is not possible to print all the products , because of the line speed and
production rate (PPM), with only one laser, Sure Shot can be used to put two laser printers working
alternately .
In this case, the Print Ready connection is not used (it must be conected to 12 VDC) and the laser
printers can be conected to stop the production line in case of faults .
It is necessary to use an external Power Supply , because Sure Shot works with 12 VDC and the laser
pinters use 24 VDC
Sure Shot needs a NPN product detector (10 to 30 VDC)
If the laser printers can only work with PNP print signal , it is necessary to convert the output signal ,
provided by Sure Shot , from NPN to PNP. It could be used an optical coupler , for example (4N25 is an
option ).
Confidential - Company Proprietary
