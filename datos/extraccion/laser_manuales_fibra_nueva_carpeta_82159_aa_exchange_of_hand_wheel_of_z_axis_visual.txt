AL-82159Service Part Installation Instruction
Exchange of Hand Wheel of Z Axis
1 Exchange of Hand Wheel of Z Axis
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be completely discon-nected!
1.Lock the hand wheel using the lever to fix the threaded spindle in position.
2.Loosen the threaded pin using a 2.5 mm Allen key.
3.Remove the hand wheel from the spindle.
4.Position the new hand wheel and screw it onto the spindle.
5.Fix the hand wheel using the threaded pin.
6.If necessary: Loosen the lever for repositioning.
