Adjustment of the Q-Switch DN-A
14.Adjust the Q-switch to its minimum power value.
15.If the measuring device indicates no power, repeat step 13.
16.Set the RF amplitude in the software to maximum and make sure that no power can be mea-sured.

18.Adjust only the screws of the end mirror using the adjusting key until the maximum outputpower is reached.
19.Mount the laser scan lens (see section "Mounting of the laser scan lens" in chapter "Main-tenance").
20.Close the housing cover of the laser head.
