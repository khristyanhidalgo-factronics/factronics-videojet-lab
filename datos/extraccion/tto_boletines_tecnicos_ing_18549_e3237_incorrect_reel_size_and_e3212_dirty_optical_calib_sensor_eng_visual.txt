1. Remove the Cassette and clean of f the LEDs on the Print Head PCB.

3. Run the test and you will see the red LEDs light up on the Printhead PCB.
If the readings are good, the LEDs will light once and the Status should come up
as V isible.
If there is an issue it will keep running the red LEDs and the Status will say
Partially or Fully Obscured.

is not reading properly . The sensor might be dirty or faulty .
