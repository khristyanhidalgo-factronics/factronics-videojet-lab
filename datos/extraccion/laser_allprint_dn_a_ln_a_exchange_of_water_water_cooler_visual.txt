Exchange of the Water/Water Cooler

1 Exchange of the Water/Water Cooler
The water/water cooler must be exchangedin case of leakage
if the following components are defective:- heat exchanger
- flow sensor- cooling water pump
- water solenoid valve- temperature controller
For exchanging the water/water cooler the following is required:hex key 5 mm
socket wrench 7 mmof the accessory kit:
hex key 3 mm (00478)insulated screwdriver (66797)
combination spanner 10 mm (66923)
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be dead!1.Turn the key switch to position »0«.
Remove the key to ensure that the laser system cannot be switched on.
2.Wait until the message »Key switch open« is displayed on the touch screen.
3.Switch off main switch.
4.Disconnect the mains supply.
ImportantBefore exchanging the water/water cooler the complete laser systemmust be drained (see section "Draining of the Laser System", chapter
"Installation").

Exchange of the Water/Water Cooler
1.1 Removal
ImportantMake sure that no dirt gets into the open cooling water hoses.
1.Remove the front panel of the PCM housing.
2.Remove the cooling water hoses of the water/water cooler:- for the marking unit (1)
- for the liquid cooler (2)- for the non DI compressor chiller or for municipal water (3)
3.Remove the three fixing screws in the bottom plate and pull out the water/water cooler unituntil the terminal box is accessible.
4.Remove the screws of the cover of the terminal box and open it.
5.Disconnect the 14-pole female connector from the CTP (Cooler Terminal PCB).
6.Loosen the locking screws of the flow sensor connector (interface X300) and, if available,of the cooler bridge (interface X301) and remove the connectors.
7.Remove the fixing screws of both female connectors and pull them backwards.
8.Remove the water/water cooler using the handles.

Exchange of the Water/Water Cooler
1.2 Mounting
1.Insert the water/water cooler into the PCM housing so that the terminal box remains acces-sible.
2.Mount the female connectors.
3.Connect the flow sensor connector and, if available, the cooler bridge to the female connec-tors.
4.Connect the 14-pole female connector to the CTP.
5.Close the cover of the terminal box and tighten the screws.
6.Push the water/water cooler completely into the PCM housing.
7.Connect all cooling water hoses to the water/water cooler (see figure on previous page).Observe the color code of the hoses (initial water: blue, return water: red)!
8.Fix the water/water cooler using the three fixing screws.
9.Mount the front panel of the PCM housing.
ImportantAfter exchanging the water/water cooler you have to carry out the start-up of the laser system (see section "Installation and Start-Up" in chap-
ter "Installation").
