Note - Measuring Printhead Gap
T o measure the printhead gap, use the printhead gap tool and ensure the printhead is over the platen pad / print roller in
the "ready to print" position.
1. Ensure the printhead gap does not exceed 5 mm . Keep in mind if trying to test print on a bench, the printhead
needs to come into contact with a platen surface as it needs to measure the printhead gap. If it senses nothing
and comes down too far (greater than 5mm), you will always get this fault.
2. Ensure the printhead gap is not less than 1mm . If it is too close to the printing surface, it could drag and cause
the printhead to not travel freely . This is especially true in intermittent applications. This printer supports a gap
between 1-5mm, with 2mm being the ideal gap setting.
3. Ensure the printer is running r12 SP5 or greater software.
4. If this is a continuous application, ensure proper print position setting over the print roller .
Print Position for Continuous Applications
While TT O T echnical Support typically recommends that the printhead be as close over the center of the roller as
possible, there are applications in which it may be required to print on a dif ferent spot on the roller .
TT O T echnical Support has found that in slow(er) applications, the printhead may need to be a few mm
in from of the center of the print roller .
If we were to say 50.00mm setting was the center of the roller , then decreasing the value would move
printhead in front of the roller .
TT O T echnical Support has also found that in fast(er) applications, the printhead may need to be
positioned slightly behind the center of the roller .
If we were to again say that 50.00mm setting is the center of the roller , then increasing the value would
move the printhead behind the roller .
An improper print position setting could cause the fault.
5. If running in continuous mode with an encoder , check that the encoder rubber O-ring is installed and intact.
6. Check the carriage for binding:
1. Power the printer OFF .
2. Remove the cassette and slide the carriage back and forth. It will have a bit of resistance but for the
most part should move free from any obstructions, debris, or binding.
3. If there is slight binding, try cleaning the linear slide by blowing it out with dry , compressed air . Y ou can
also lubricate the slide using the VJ linear slide lubrication kit (P/N: 4021 13).
4. Check the carriage position belt pully for binding or wear .
7. Inspect the peel roller shaft and peel arm assembly ensuring they are not bent as this could keep the printhead
from coming down unobstructed. Below is a picture of a correct peel arm assembly parallel with the carriage

assembly . Replace if bent. Note: The following would apply to printers with serial numbers before
182401 1002TZH. The design was changed later .
8. Inspect the entirety of the belts for wear or physical damage, ensuring you pay close attention to the belt section
around the clamps. W orn/damaged belts in the clamp area can/will cause the carriage to slip which can/will raise
the fault. Replace as necessary .
Note that there is a second clamp located under the carriage assembly .

9. Ensure all of the magnets on the printhead mount and the printhead pivot assembly are in place and not loose or
damaged/cracked.
10. Inspect the 2-piece printhead belt clamp and the carriage belt clamp to ensure they are fully clamped and flush
with one another . The clamp is secured with 2 Phillips screws. A belt on a loose clamp can cause the belt to slip
and potentially raise the fault.

tight (bottom).

screws (Figure 2) or dowel pins (Figure 3).

12. If you hear a grinding noise or if the carriage does not move freely across the slide, inspect the pulleys and
ensure no physical damage or that they have seized. If seized, replace the pulleys.
13. If you hear a grinding noise and the pulleys are fine, inspect the printhead motor connections on the main
PCB ensuring the connectors on the main PCB are fully seated and also that the connections on the motors are
plugged into the correct connectors on the main PCB. The motor connections need to be connected into the
connectors on the main PCB closest to the motors (see Figure 4). TIP: The motor that has the encoder on it
always plugs into X27.
Grinding Noises Unrelated to Hand Setting
In previous TT O printers, a grinding noise followed by the fault could be caused by the printer hand being set
incorrectly . With the 6330/6530, the hand is automatically set/detected by the printhead PCB. Y ou can verify if it
is correct by going to T ools | Diagnostics | Printhead | General .

Figure 4: Correct printhead motor and printhead encoder connections.
14. Investigate whether the fault occurred immediately after the ribbon ran out. It is possible for this fault to occur
ifthe ribbon leader breaks free from the ribbon core when the reel has finished. The leader can then 'stick' to and
tug the printhead, which could raise the fault. However , in this case there is no real negative impact because
when the ribbon is replaced and the "End of Reel" fault is cleared, the "Printhead Carriage Position Fault" can
also be cleared.
15. If this is an intermittent application, ensure the film is at a complete standstill at all times when printing. If the
substrate begins to advance as the printer is still printing, the substrate can tug/pull the printhead, which could
then raise the fault.
16. If at power up, the printer makes a loud grinding/buzzing noise, the carriage moves erratically , and then the
printhead extends out, then the issue is likely due to the main printer PCB.
17. If all the above checks out fine, the next option is to tension the belt. This is only to be carried out by VJ Service
personnel.
Follow the tensioning instructions as detailed in Belt T ensioning Instructions.PDF
Y ou will need V ideojet belt tension service tool.
18. If all of the previous check out fine, and re-tensioning the belt does not resolve the issue, it could be a faulty
printhead motor w/encoder sensor (32mm/53mm/107mm printers) or it could be a faulty carriage motor
w/encoder sensor (107mm printer). These tend to be very reliable parts and you should only
attempt replacement if you are absolutely certain nothing else listed here was the issue.
