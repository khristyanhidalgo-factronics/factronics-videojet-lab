7.Mount the three grounding cables to the grounding bolt.
8.Connect the cables to the secondary side of the 350 W module.Make sure that the feedback lines are mounted in V-shape.

12. If the system is equipped with CPD3 and FASS (instead of SPM):Insert the FASS on its mounting plate and tighten the four fixing screws.
13.Insert the mounting base back into the housing and push it to the back.
