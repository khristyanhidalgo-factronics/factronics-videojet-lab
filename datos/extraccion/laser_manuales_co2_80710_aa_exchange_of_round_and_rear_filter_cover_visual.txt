AL-80710Service Part Installation Instruction
Exchange of the Filter Covers
1 Exchange of the Filter Covers
1.1 Round Front Filter
1.Open the round front filter by inserting a lean screwdriver from below into the groove andlevering off the cover.
2.Snap the new round cover back in place. Make sure the groove is pointing downwards.
1.2 Rear Filter
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be completely discon-nected!
1.Open the rear filter cover by removing the screw with a 2.5mm Allen key. Then push thecover upwards and pull it out.
2.Mount the new filter cover with mat onto the rear by inserting the cover and tightening thefixing screw.
