5. Start by stripping back the outer cable to expose the Shielding, then separate the individual wires and

6. Roll the shielding back onto the cable which allows for contact with the DIN connector or shape the
shielding for installing in Chameleon Customer Interface X21 connector .
7. Using the 0.9 millimeter Allen wrench, back of f the screws to prepare for installation of each wire.

9. Once the shielding is in place and the wires are securely connected, then begin to reassemble the DIN
connector .
