Related Technical Escalations
TS25092521245 - Incorrect Date Offset with CMark 5.4
Global Laser Technical Support has identified an issue with CMark version 5.4 and
5.4.1. Specifically, expiration or best- by dates set between December 29 to Decem -
ber 31 are incorrectly calculating the dates in the following year.
The issue occurs with year- end dates in specific years, including 2025, 2029, 2030,
2031, 2035, and 2036.
CMark 5.4 was introduced on July 21, 2025; only laser systems that were shipped
between July 21 and October 1, or had CMark 5.4 installed during this range, are
affected.
Required Downgrade for Affected Customers
Field Service Engineers should proactively downgrade CMark version 5.4 for
customers affected by the date offset issue, following the instructions in
Corrective Action .

Corrective Action
1. Download CMark 5.2.3 and 5.3.1. Software is available at Service Resources |
Service Software | Laser | CMark | CMark Software - Multi- Core .
2. Perform a backup on the current interface (TCS+ or SmartGraph). This is
necessary because after the downgrade, no files will be available.
3. Install the applicable version, depending on the printer model:
l
CMark 5.2.3  - 3140, 3350, 3640, 7230, 7330, 7340, 7440, 7510, 7610, or
7810
l
CMark 5.3.1 - 7920
4. Upload the backup to the correct interface, either TCS+ or SmartGraph.
5. When closing Mandatory Downgrade Work Orders , close the work order
with one of the following results, as appropriate:
l
Upgrade Complete
l
Customer Rejected Upgrade
l
Equipment not operable/present
l
Other - See Notes
What is Being Done to Find a Permanent Solution
l
MOL Engineering has identified the root cause as a previous fix in CMark 5.4
related to the calendar week bug. Developers are actively working on a
resolution.
l
Systems being shipped from WD manufacturing sites beginning October 2,
2025, will have laser systems with CMark 5.2.3 installed for CO2/Fiber or CMark
5.3.1 for 7920 UV.
l
Systems being shipped from SD/ZH manufacturing sites beginning October 3,
2025, will have laser systems with CMark 5.2.3 installed for CO2/Fiber or CMark
5.3.1 for 7920 UV.
l
Field service is advised to downgrade systems for customers who use this date
offset that could result in a calculated date between December 29 and
December 31, 2025.
