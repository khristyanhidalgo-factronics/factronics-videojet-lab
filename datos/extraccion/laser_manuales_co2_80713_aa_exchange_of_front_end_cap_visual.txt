AL-80713Service Part Installation Instruction
Exchange of Front End Cap
1 Exchange of Front End Cap
DangerBefore starting any work the laser system must be completely discon-nected!
1.Place the dust cover onto the focus module.
2.Carefully remove the focus module and put it in a clean place.
3.Remove the side covers from the front cap by pressing with a finger into the recess andremove the covers to the side.
4.Remove the round cover by inserting a lean screwdriver from below into the groove and le-vering off the cover (see figure below). Keep it for the new cap.
5.Remove the front cap using a 4 mm hex key.
